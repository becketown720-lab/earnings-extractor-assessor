EXHIBIT 99.1
RALPH LAUREN REPORTS SECOND QUARTER FISCAL 2027 RESULTS AHEAD OF EXPECTATIONS AND RAISES FISCAL 2027 OUTLOOK
•Second Quarter Revenue Increased 9% on a Reported Basis and 10% in Constant Currency, Exceeding Expectations, with Every Region Contributing to Growth led by Asia
•Global Direct-to-Consumer Comparable Store Sales Increased 10%, with Balanced Growth Across Digital and Brick-and-Mortar Channels with 9% Average Unit Retail ("AUR") Growth
•Adjusted Gross and Operating Margin Expansion Exceeded Our Outlook, Driven by Strong Full-Price Demand and Expense Discipline, More Than Offsetting Continued Investments in Marketing and Long-Term Growth Initiatives
•Reinforced Balance Sheet Strength with $1.8 Billion in Cash and Short-Term Investments and Inventories Well-Positioned to Global Demand
•Returned More Than $600 Million to Shareholders Through Our Dividend and Repurchase of Class A Common Stock This Fiscal Year-to-Date
•Raised Full Year Fiscal 2027 Constant Currency Revenue and Adjusted Operating Margin Expansion Outlook, Reflecting Stronger Than Expected First Half Fiscal Year Performance
NEW YORK -- (BUSINESS WIRE) -- November 5, 2026 -- Ralph Lauren Corporation (NYSE:RL), a global leader in the design, marketing, and distribution of luxury lifestyle products, today reported earnings per diluted share of $4.87, up 47% to prior year on a reported basis and $4.64, up 22% on an adjusted basis, excluding restructuring-related and other net benefits, for the second quarter of Fiscal 2027. This compared to earnings per diluted share of $3.32 on a reported basis and $3.79 on an adjusted basis, excluding restructuring-related and other net charges, for the second quarter of Fiscal 2026.

"As we enter the Holiday season, I'm reminded of what has always inspired me — the beauty of the world around us, the warmth of family and the optimism and sense of possibility that have guided us for nearly 60 years," said Ralph Lauren, Executive Chairman and Chief Creative Officer. "These values are deeply personal to me and remain at the heart of our Company and everything we create."

"We delivered strong second quarter performance, enabling us to raise our Fiscal 2027 outlook once again," said Patrice Louvet, President and Chief Executive Officer. "Through a dynamic global operating environment, we continue to invest in the strategic priorities that are bringing our Next Great Chapter: Drive plan to life — strengthening our brand desirability and delighting multi-generational customers in every geography around the world to drive sustainable long-term growth and value creation. We remain confident in the strength of our brand, our teams and our diversified engines of growth through this fiscal year and beyond."

Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Income Statement Review
Net Revenue. In the second quarter of Fiscal 2027, revenue increased 9% to $2.2 billion on a reported basis and was up 10% in constant currency. Foreign currency negatively impacted revenue growth by approximately 50 basis points in the second quarter.
Revenue performance for the Company's reportable segments in the second quarter compared to the prior year period was as follows:
•North America Revenue. North America revenue in the second quarter increased 6% to $885 million on a reported basis. In retail, comparable store sales in North America increased 6%, with a 6% increase in brick and mortar stores and a 5% increase in digital commerce. North America wholesale revenue increased 8% to the prior year, including approximately 4 points of benefit from earlier timing of shipments from the second half of Fiscal 2027.
2


•Europe Revenue. Europe revenue in the second quarter increased 1% to $696 million on a reported basis. In constant currency, revenue increased 3%. In retail, comparable store sales in Europe decreased 1%, with a 4% decrease in brick and mortar stores and a 9% increase in digital commerce. Europe wholesale revenue increased 3% to prior year on a reported basis and increased 4% in constant currency.    
•Asia Revenue. Asia revenue in the second quarter increased 28% to $571 million on a reported basis. In constant currency, revenue also increased 28%. Comparable store sales in Asia increased 24%, with a 23% increase in brick and mortar stores and a 31% increase in digital commerce.
Gross Profit. Gross profit for the second quarter of Fiscal 2027 was $1.6 billion and gross margin was 74.2%, 620 basis points above the prior year. Gross margin expansion on a reported basis was primarily driven by tariff refunds received in the quarter. On an adjusted basis, gross profit for the second quarter of Fiscal 2027 was $1.5 billion and gross margin was 70.5%, 250 basis points above the prior year. Gross margin expansion on an adjusted basis was driven by AUR growth, favorable geographic mix, and lower product costs.
Operating Expenses. Operating expenses in the second quarter of Fiscal 2027 were $1.3 billion, up 13% to last year on a reported basis. On an adjusted basis, operating expenses were $1.2 billion, up 11% to last year. Adjusted operating expense rate was 54.8%, compared to 53.9% in the prior year period.
Operating Income. Operating income for the second quarter of Fiscal 2027 was $364 million and operating margin was 16.6% on a reported basis. On an adjusted basis, operating income was $345 million and operating margin was 15.7%, 160 basis points above the prior year. Operating income for the Company's reportable segments in the second quarter compared to the prior year period was as follows:
•North America Operating Income. North America operating income in the second quarter was $186 million and operating margin was 21.0%, up 160 basis points to last year.
•Europe Operating Income. Europe operating income in the second quarter was $204 million and operating margin was 29.4%, flat to last year. Foreign currency benefited operating margin rate by 80 basis points in the second quarter.
•Asia Operating Income. Asia operating income in the second quarter was $157 million and operating margin was 27.4%, up 240 basis points to last year. Foreign currency negatively impacted operating margin rate by 10 basis points in the second quarter.
Net Income and EPS. Net income in the second quarter of Fiscal 2027 was $297 million, or $4.87 per diluted share on a reported basis. On an adjusted basis, net income was $283 million, or $4.64 per diluted share. This compared to net income of $207 million, or $3.32 per diluted share on a reported basis, and net income of $237 million, or $3.79 per diluted share on an adjusted basis, for the second quarter of Fiscal 2026.
In the second quarter of Fiscal 2027, the Company had an effective tax rate of approximately 20% on a reported basis and 19% on an adjusted basis, in-line with its outlook. This compared to an effective tax rate of approximately 15% on a reported basis and 16% on an adjusted basis in the prior year period. The increase was driven primarily by the absence of favorable discrete tax benefits realized in the prior year period.

Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Review
The Company ended the second quarter of Fiscal 2027 with $1.8 billion in cash and short-term investments and $1.2 billion in total debt, compared to $1.6 billion and $1.2 billion, respectively, at the end of the second quarter of Fiscal 2026. Inventory at the end of the second quarter of Fiscal 2027 was $1.2 billion, down 9% compared to the prior year period.
The Company repurchased approximately $250 million of Class A Common Stock in the second quarter for a total of $500 million this fiscal year-to-date, completing the Company's share repurchase plans for Fiscal 2027.

Full Year Fiscal 2027 and Third Quarter Outlook
The Company's outlook is based on its best assessment of the current geopolitical and macroeconomic environment, including tariffs, inflationary pressures and other consumer spending-related headwinds, global supply chain disruptions, and foreign currency volatility, among other factors. The full year Fiscal 2027 and third quarter guidance excludes any potential restructuring-related and other net charges that may be incurred in future periods, as described in the "Non-U.S. GAAP Financial Measures" section of this press release.
For Fiscal 2027, the Company expects constant currency revenues to increase mid- to high-single digits to last year on a 52-week comparable basis, now centered around 6% to 7%, up from a range of 5% to 6% previously, reflecting better-than-expected second quarter results. Based on current exchange rates, foreign currency is now expected to negatively impact revenue growth by approximately 40 to 60 basis points in Fiscal 2027.
The Company now expects operating margin for Fiscal 2027 to expand approximately 80 to 100 basis points in constant currency, up from its prior outlook, driven primarily by gross margin expansion. Gross and operating margin expansion are still expected to be stronger in the first half of the fiscal year, largely due to the timing of key marketing activations compared to the prior year period and previously-disclosed shifts in the timing of wholesale shipments. Foreign currency is now expected to benefit gross margin by approximately 10 to 30 basis points and have a roughly neutral impact on operating margin in Fiscal 2027.
Fiscal 2027 is a 53-week year, with the 53rd week expected to contribute an additional 1 point to revenue growth and benefit operating margin slightly for the full fiscal year.
For the third quarter, the Company expects revenues to grow approximately low- to mid-single digits on a constant currency basis, centered around 3% to 4%. This outlook includes approximately 1 point of negative impact from wholesale timing shifts and strategic off-price reductions in the quarter. Foreign currency is expected to negatively impact revenue growth by approximately 50 to 100 basis points.
Operating margin for the third quarter is expected to expand approximately 50 to 70 basis points in constant currency, with gross margin expansion more than offsetting higher marketing investments in the holiday period. Foreign currency is expected to benefit gross margin by about 20 to 40 basis points and have a roughly neutral to 20 basis point benefit to operating margin in the quarter.
The Company's full year Fiscal 2027 tax rate is still expected to be in the range of approximately 21% to 22%. The third quarter tax rate is expected to be approximately 23% to 24%.

ABOUT RALPH LAUREN
Ralph Lauren Corporation (NYSE:RL) is a global leader in the design, marketing and distribution of luxury lifestyle products in six categories: apparel, handbags, footwear & accessories, fragrances, home, and hospitality. For nearly 60 years, Ralph Lauren has sought to inspire the dream of a better life through authenticity and timeless style. Its reputation and distinctive image have been developed across a wide range of products, brands, distribution channels and international markets. The Company's brand names — which include Ralph Lauren, Ralph Lauren Collection, Ralph Lauren Purple Label, Double RL, Polo Ralph Lauren, Lauren Ralph Lauren, RLX Ralph Lauren, Polo Ralph Lauren Children and Chaps, among others — constitute one of the world's most widely recognized families of consumer brands. NON-U.S. GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES
Because Ralph Lauren Corporation is a global company, the comparability of its operating results reported in U.S. Dollars is affected by foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations because the underlying currencies in which it transacts change in value over time compared to the U.S. Dollar. Such fluctuations can have a significant effect on the Company's reported results. As such, in addition to financial measures prepared in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the U.S. ("U.S. GAAP"), the Company's discussions often contain references to constant currency measures, which are calculated by translating current-year and prior-year reported amounts into comparable amounts using a single foreign exchange rate for each currency. The Company presents constant currency financial information, which is a non-U.S. GAAP financial measure, as a supplement to its reported operating results. The Company uses constant currency information to provide a framework for assessing how its businesses performed excluding the effects of foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations. Management believes this information is useful to investors for facilitating comparisons of operating results and better identifying trends in the Company's businesses. The constant currency performance measures should be viewed in addition to, and not in lieu of or superior to, the Company's operating performance measures calculated in accordance with U.S. GAAP.
This earnings release also includes certain other non-U.S. GAAP financial measures relating to the impact of charges and other items as described herein. The Company uses non-U.S. GAAP financial measures, among other things, to evaluate its operating performance and to better represent the manner in which it conducts and views its business. The Company believes that excluding items that are not comparable from period to period helps investors and others compare operating performance between two periods. While the Company considers non-U.S. GAAP measures useful in analyzing its results, they are not intended to replace, nor act as a substitute for, any presentation included in the consolidated financial statements

Adjustments made during the fiscal periods presented include charges recorded in connection with the Company's restructuring activities, as well as certain other charges (benefits) associated with other non-recurring events, as described in the footnotes to the non-U.S. GAAP financial measures above. Included in this earnings release are reconciliations between the non-U.S. GAAP financial measures and the most directly comparable U.S. GAAP measures before and after these adjustments.
