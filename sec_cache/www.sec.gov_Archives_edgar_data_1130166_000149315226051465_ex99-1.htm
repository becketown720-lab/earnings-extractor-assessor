BIO GREEN MED SOLUTION Reports THIRD quarter financial results and provides business update

 

KUALA LUMPUR, MALAYSIA, November 12, 2026 – Bio Green Med Solution, Inc. (NASDAQ: BGMS; “BGMS” or the “Company”), a diversified company engaged primarily in the provision of fire safety protection and distribution activities, today announced third quarter financial results and provided a business update.

 

Highlights of the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, or in some cases shortly thereafter, include:

 

●In October, the Company’s previously filed Form S-4, detailing the Company’s proposed Business Combination Agreement with Future NRG Sdn. Bhd., a Malaysia private limited company, was declared effective. The Company is planning to hold a Special Meeting in November 2026 to obtain shareholder approval for the proposed Business Combination.
●In October, the Board of Directors of the Company declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.15 per share on the Company’s 6% Convertible Exchangeable Preferred Stock, which cash dividend was paid on November 1, 2026, to holders of record as of the close of business on October 26, 2026.

 

“During the third quarter, our fire safety business generated $454,000 in product revenue, compared to $81,000 in the prior-year period, and yielded a gross margin of approximately 25%, while our net loss narrowed to $0.4 million from $1.0 million in the prior-year period,” said Datuk Dr. Doris Wong Sing Ee, Chief Executive Officer and Executive Director. “We also reduced general and administrative expenses by 37% year over year and ended the quarter with $3.5 million in cash and cash equivalents. We remain focused on executing in Malaysia’s fire safety market and on working toward the completion of our proposed business combination with Future NRG, which remains subject to the closing conditions and termination rights set forth in the Business Combination Agreement.”

 

Financial Highlights

 

As of September 30, 2026, cash and cash equivalents totaled $3.5 million, compared to $3.5 million as of December 31, 2025.

 

Net cash used in operating activities was $0.9 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026. The Company estimates that its current cash resources will fund planned expenditure into the second quarter of 2027.

 

Following the acquisition of Fitters Sdn. Bhd. on September 12, 2025, product revenue from sales and distribution of fire safety equipment was $454,000 for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $81,000 for the same period in the prior year.

 

Cost of sales related to sales and distribution of fire safety equipment was $339,000 for the three months ended September 30, 2026, yielding a gross margin of approximately 25%, compared to $64,000 for the same period in the prior year, yielding a gross margin of approximately 21%.

 

 
 

 

General and administrative expenses decreased by approximately $0.4 million from $1.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025 to $0.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, due to regulatory filings and acquisition costs associated with the Fitters acquisition in the prior period.

 

Total other income, net, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, increased by approximately $70,000 from $4,000 for the three months ended September 30, 2025 to $74,000 for the three months ended September 30, 2026, due largely to interest income.

 

Income tax provision for the three months ended September 30, 2026, increased by approximately $3,000, from $0 for the three months ended September 30, 2025 to $3,000 for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and related to our fire safety business, acquired in September 2025.

 

Net loss from discontinued operations was $5,000 for the three months ended September 30, 2025 and is related to our former operations from biopharmaceutical activities. There was no activity from discontinued operations in the current period.

 

Net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was $0.4 million, compared to $1.0 million for the same period in 2025.

 

About Bio Green Med Solution, Inc.

 

BGMS is a diversified company that was formerly engaged in the biopharmaceutical industry but as of September 2025 has shifted its operations to focus on provision of fire safety protection and distribution activities. Specifically, on September 12, 2025, the Company completed its acquisition of Fitters Sdn. Bhd., a Malaysia-based group specializing in fire protection products and services. The Company is now focused on advancing opportunities across these distinct sectors whilst maintaining its commitment to driving long-term value creation for shareholders. For additional information, please visit www.bgmsglobal.com.
