Regional Management Corp. Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results

- Net income of $15.1 million and diluted earnings per share of $1.61, up 5.3% and 13.4% year-over-year, respectively -

- Year-to-date net income and diluted earnings per share up 10.0% and 15.2% year-
over-year, respectively -

- Record total revenue of $178.8 million, up 8.0% year-over-year, on portfolio growth and bank partnership fee income -

- Annualized operating expense ratio improved 20 basis points year-over-year -

Greenville, South Carolina – October 28, 2026 – Regional Management Corp. (NYSE: RM), a diversified consumer finance company, today announced results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“Our third quarter results reflect steady execution against the priorities we outlined in July,” said Lakhbir S. Lamba, President and Chief Executive Officer of Regional Management Corp. “We grew the portfolio in line with our expectations, generated record revenue supported by fee income from our bank partnership, and earned net income of $15.1 million, or $1.61 per diluted share, while maintaining disciplined expense management.”

 

“Our net credit loss rate for the third quarter of 2026 was 10.9%, a seasonal improvement of 130 basis points sequentially and up 70 basis points from the prior-year period,” continued Mr. Lamba. “We have identified the drivers of the year-over-year increase and have actions in place against each. We tightened underwriting in the segments that underperformed and began implementing enhanced fraud controls across all channels. In the fourth quarter, we will also complete the rollout of our new proprietary origination scorecard, and Liz Canini will join us in early November as Chief Credit Risk Officer to lead our credit organization.”

 

“Our bank partnership is becoming central to how we compete,” added Mr. Lamba. “Since we last reported, we have extended the program to three additional states, and we continue to expand it across our footprint. The partnership allows us to further refine our pricing strategy to support growth, credit performance, and attractive risk-adjusted returns over time. Together with disciplined underwriting and our investments in talent, technology, and data, it positions Regional for more consistent growth and higher returns, including strong growth on our bottom line in 2027.”

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

 

•
Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $15.1 million and diluted earnings per share was $1.61, up 5.3% and 13.4% year-over-year, respectively.

 

•
Net finance receivables as of September 30, 2026 were $2.2 billion, an increase of $156.9 million, or 7.6%, from the prior-year period and $61.7 million from June 30, 2026, driven by growth in large loans, including auto-secured loans, and 14 new branches opened since September 30, 2025.

 

o
Total originations of $530.3 million increased 5.3% sequentially and 1.5% from the prior-year period. Large loan originations increased 11.8% from the prior-year period, while small loan originations decreased 21.9%, reflecting tighter underwriting in higher-risk segments.

 

o
Large loan net finance receivables of $1.7 billion increased $229.9 million, or 15.2%, from the prior-year period and represented 78.8% of the total loan portfolio, compared to 73.7% in the prior-year period.

 

▪
Auto-secured net finance receivables of $345.0 million increased $69.5 million, or 25.2%, from the prior-year period and represented 15.6% of the total loan portfolio, compared to 13.4% in the prior-year period.

 

o
Small loan net finance receivables of $467.9 million decreased $73.0 million, or 13.5%, from the prior-year period and represented 21.2% of the total loan portfolio, compared to 26.3% in the prior-year period.

 

•
Record quarterly total revenue of $178.8 million, an increase of $13.3 million, or 8.0%, from the prior-year period, primarily due to growth in average net finance receivables and an increase in fee income from bank partnership originations.

 

o
Total revenue yield (annualized total revenue as a percentage of average net finance receivables) for the third quarter of 2026 was 32.9%, increasing 110 basis points sequentially, driven by fee income from bank partnership originations and seasonality. Total revenue yield decreased 20 basis points from the prior-year period, as lower interest and fee yield and lower insurance income were largely offset by approximately 120 basis points of fee income from bank partnership originations.

 

o
Interest and fee yield (annualized interest and fee income as a percentage of average net finance receivables) for the third quarter of 2026 was 28.7%, compared to 29.7% in the prior-year period, a decrease of 100 basis points primarily due to product mix shift toward larger and auto-secured loans, which carry lower yields

and lower loss rates, as well as pricing changes and the impact of higher credit losses on revenue.

 

•
Provision for credit losses for the third quarter of 2026 was $66.2 million, an increase of $5.7 million, or 9.4%, from the prior-year period, driven by higher net credit losses, partially offset by a smaller reserve build than in the prior-year period.

 

o
The net credit loss rate (annualized net credit losses as a percentage of average net finance receivables) for the third quarter of 2026 was 10.9%, a 130 basis point decrease sequentially and a 70 basis point increase compared to 10.2% in the prior-year period. The year-over-year increase was concentrated in specific segments of the portfolio, primarily loans originated in 2025, with the digital affiliate channel being the largest driver.

 

o
The provision for credit losses for the third quarter of 2026 included a sequential reserve increase of $6.8 million, primarily due to portfolio growth occurring during the third quarter of 2026.

 

o
The allowance for credit losses was $230.8 million as of September 30, 2026, or 10.4% of net finance receivables, consistent sequentially and up 10 basis points compared to 10.3% in the prior-year period.

 

•
As of September 30, 2026, 30+ day contractual delinquencies totaled $165.8 million, or 7.5% of net finance receivables, a 50 basis point increase both sequentially and year-over-year.

 

o
The 30+ day contractual delinquency rate on the company’s higher-quality auto-secured portfolio was 2.2% as of September 30, 2026.

 

•
General and administrative expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $68.6 million, an increase of $4.6 million from the prior-year period. The operating expense ratio (annualized general and administrative expenses as a percentage of average net finance receivables) for the third quarter of 2026 was 12.6%, an improvement of 20 basis points from 12.8% in the prior-year period, despite approximately 40 basis points of upfront origination costs associated with bank partnership loans.

 

•
In the third quarter of 2026, the company repurchased 265,645 shares of its common stock at a weighted-average price of $37.64 per share under the company’s stock repurchase program.

Strategic Highlights

 

During the third quarter, the company continued to expand its bank partnership program with Column N.A., a nationally chartered bank, under which Column has originated more than $160 million in loans since the program’s launch in March 2026. Following full implementation of the program for loans made through the company’s branches in Texas, its largest state, the company has extended the program to North Carolina, Louisiana, and Missouri, and it continues to expect substantially all of its branch network to operate under the program by the end of 2027. In addition to generating fee income for the company, the program enables more risk-aligned loan pricing, which the company believes will reduce adverse selection over time.

 

The company also advanced initiatives to strengthen credit performance and operating efficiency. Enhanced fraud controls introduced in June include third-party fraud scores, step-up identity verification, and a dedicated central fraud operations team, and the company’s new proprietary origination scorecard is now live in the majority of its states. The company’s new branch loan origination platform will be live in all branches during the fourth quarter. In collections, the company has centralized the management of late-stage delinquent accounts, enabling its branches to focus on sales and early-stage delinquency, and has deployed enhanced contact data, branded calling, and AI-enabled tools to improve customer engagement, cure rates, and collection efficiency.

 

In July 2026, the company launched end-to-end digital lending, enabling customers to complete the entire loan process online. The company is scaling the channel with discipline, expanding only as credit performance validates its risk-adjusted return expectations. Over time, the company expects digital originations to complement its branch network, which remains at the core of its operations, and to become an important contributor to growth.

About Regional Management Corp.

 

Regional Management Corp. (NYSE: RM) is a diversified consumer finance company that provides attractive, easy-to-understand installment loan products primarily to customers with limited access to consumer credit from banks, thrifts, credit card companies, and other lenders. Regional Management operates under the name “Regional Finance” online and in branch locations in 20 states across the United States. Each of its loan products is structured on a fixed-rate, fixed-term basis with fully amortizing equal monthly installment payments, repayable at any time without penalty. Regional Management sources loans through its multiple channel platform, which includes branches, centrally managed direct mail campaigns, digital partners, and its consumer website. For more information, please visit www.RegionalManagement.com.
