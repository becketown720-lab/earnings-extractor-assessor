ARS Pharma Reports Business Highlights and Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Total Q3 revenue of $37.7 million, including $33.4 million in U.S. net product revenue for neffy®

 

neffy U.S. market share reached 5% overall and 9% in field-targeted accounts, supporting the company’s updated targeted provider commercial strategy


Reduced SG&A spend reflected in Q3 operating expenses; reaffirm path to cash flow breakeven by year-end 2027

 

Leadership team strengthened with appointments of Randall Kaye, M.D., as Chief Medical Officer and John Slebir as Chief Legal Officer

 

Conference call and webcast today at 4:30 p.m. ET / 1:30 p.m. PT

 

SAN DIEGO, Nov. 5, 2026 -- ARS Pharmaceuticals (Nasdaq: SPRY), a commercial biopharmaceutical company focused on needle-free epinephrine, today reported its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, as well as recent corporate and commercial highlights.

 

“In August, we committed to three corporate priorities – steady market share gains with neffy, greater financial discipline with a path to cash flow breakeven by year-end 2027, and expansion of our needle-free epinephrine platform beyond anaphylaxis. I’m proud to report that our team has delivered on each,” said Donn Casale, President and Chief Executive Officer of ARS Pharma. “Market share grew where our field team engaged; operating expenses were in line with guidance, including a 42% reduction in third-quarter SG&A expenses from the first-half 2026 quarterly average; and ARS-2 is on track for interim Phase 2b data in the first quarter of 2027.

 

“These results build the foundation for our future-looking three-pillar growth strategy – capture market share with neffy in our base market; expand the epinephrine market over time; and extend our platform, starting with our chronic spontaneous urticaria. With new leadership in place across our commercial, medical and legal functions, we have the team, the strategy and the discipline to execute. The opportunity in front of us is significant, and we believe we are well positioned to pursue it with focus and urgency.”

 

 

 

Third Quarter 2026 neffy Commercial Highlights

•
Market share: Total U.S. market share reached 5% in the third quarter of 2026, up from 4% in the third quarter of 2025.
•
Field-targeted market share: Market share among field-targeted accounts, which represent approximately 44% of the total market opportunity, reached approximately 9%, up from 6% for the same period in 2025. By comparison, market share was 2% in non-targeted accounts in the third quarter of 2026.
•
Net U.S. product revenue: U.S. net product revenue for neffy of $33.4 million, compared with $31.3 million in the third quarter of 2025.
•
Prescriber base: 19,605 total unique prescribers in the third quarter of 2026, up 49% compared with the third quarter of 2025, for a total of more than 44,000 prescribers since launch.

 

“neffy is a highly differentiated product in a large market with genuine demand, and I am confident it can become the standard of care in the epinephrine market,” said Meg Smith, Chief Commercial Officer. “The data show that when we engage providers directly, neffy wins. In my first two months, we completed a comprehensive assessment of our commercial operations, and we are taking decisive action across multiple fronts, including implementing a shift to account-based targeting, sharper provider-focused messaging, and a strengthened market access strategy. We are moving with urgency, and I expect the progress will continue to be reflected in our results, with steady gains over time.”

 

 

 

Business Updates

Leadership Appointments

ARS further strengthened its leadership team to execute its updated corporate and commercial strategy. In addition to the appointment of Meg Smith as Chief Commercial Officer in August 2026, the company also appointed:

•
Randall Kaye, M.D., M.P.H, Chief Medical Officer (CMO). Dr. Kaye brings more than 30 years of experience in clinical development, medical affairs, and regulatory strategy. He has served as CMO at multiple biopharmaceutical companies, most recently at Longboard Pharmaceuticals, where he led clinical development of bexicaserin (LP352) and supported the company through its $2.6 billion acquisition by Lundbeck in 2024. Earlier, as CMO of Avanir Pharmaceuticals, he designed and implemented the strategy that secured FDA approval of Nuedexta®. He began his industry career at Pfizer Inc., where he led late-stage clinical development of Zyrtec®and supported its U.S. launch. Dr. Kaye completed a fellowship in allergy and immunology at Harvard Medical School and a residency in pediatrics at the University of Massachusetts Medical School. He received his M.D. and M.P.H. from The George Washington University School of Medicine.
•
John Slebir, Chief Legal Officer and Corporate Secretary. Mr. Slebir brings more than 20 years of experience advising life sciences companies on commercialization, financings, strategic transactions, and governance. Most recently, he served as Senior Vice President, General Counsel, Corporate Secretary and Chief Trading and Compliance Officer at Dynavax Technologies, where he led legal support for the company’s $2.2 billion acquisition by Sanofi in 2026. Previously, he spent more than a decade at VIVUS, Inc., including as Senior Vice President, General Counsel and Corporate Secretary.

 

Pipeline Expansion

ARS-2 is progressing in a Phase 2b trial evaluating 0.5 mg and 1.0 mg doses of needle-free epinephrine for the on-demand treatment of breakthrough flares in patients with chronic spontaneous urticaria (CSU). An estimated two million Americans live with CSU, and no FDA-approved therapy is indicated to stop an acute flare in progress. Interim data from the Phase 2b trial are expected in the first quarter of 2027, with full data later in 2027, which will inform the design of a potential Phase 3 registration program.

 

 

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

•
Revenue: Total revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $37.7 million, consisting of U.S. net product revenue for neffy of $33.4 million, supply revenue of $2.6 million and collaboration revenue of $1.7 million. This compares with total revenue of $32.5 million for the third quarter of 2025, which included U.S. net product revenue of $31.3 million.
•
Total Operating Expenses: Third-quarter 2026 total operating expenses were $71.9 million, including stock-based compensation of $8.1 million. Spending is tracking in line with second-half 2026 guidance of $114 million to $126 million in aggregate SG&A and R&D expenses, including stock-based compensation.
o
SG&A Expenses: Third-quarter 2026 SG&A expenses were $43.5 million, compared with $74.8 million for the third quarter of 2025, primarily reflecting the discontinuation of linear TV and broad consumer advertising, along with more focused commercial investment.
o
R&D Expenses: Third-quarter 2026 R&D expenses were $18.3 million, primarily related to a one-time $12.5 million payment related to a license agreement for exclusive worldwide rights to certain intellectual property, as well as advancing the Phase 2b trial of ARS-2 in breakthrough flares of CSU, compared with $2.8 million for the third quarter of 2025.
•
Net Loss: Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $35.6 million, or $(0.36) per share, compared with a net loss of $51.2 million, or $(0.52) per share, for the third quarter of 2025.
•
Cash Position: As of September 30, 2026, ARS had cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments of $138.4 million. Based on its current expense base plan, the company continues to expect to reach cash flow breakeven by the end of 2027.

About ARS Pharmaceuticals, Inc.

ARS Pharma is a biopharmaceutical company developing and commercializing treatments that help at-risk patients and their caregivers treat and prevent severe allergic reactions. The company is commercializing neffy® (epinephrine nasal spray), the only needle-free epinephrine approved by the U.S. Food & Drug Administration, for the emergency treatment of Type I allergic reactions, including anaphylaxis, in adults and children weighing 33 lbs. (15 kg) or more. In the European Union, the product is marketed as EURneffy®, the only needle-free epinephrine product authorized by the European Commission, for the emergency treatment of allergic reactions, including anaphylaxis, in adults and children four years of age and older weighing 15 kg or more. The product is also marketed as 优敏速® in China. Leveraging its intranasal technology, ARS Pharma is also developing ARS-2, currently in a Phase 2b clinical trial, as a potential first treatment for acute breakthrough flares of chronic spontaneous urticaria (CSU). For more information, visit www.ars-pharma.com and follow us on LinkedIn.
