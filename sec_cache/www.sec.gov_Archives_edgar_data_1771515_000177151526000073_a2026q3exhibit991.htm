Grocery Outlet Holding Corp. Announces Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Financial Results
Emeryville, CA – November 10, 2026 – Grocery Outlet Holding Corp. (NASDAQ: GO) ("Grocery Outlet," the "Company," "we" or "our") today announced financial results for the third quarter of fiscal 2026 ended October 3, 2026.
Highlights for Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 as compared to the Prior Year Period:
•Net sales increased by 0.9% to $1.18 billion.
•Comparable store sales increased by 0.5%.
•Gross margin was 30.0% compared to 30.4% last year.
•Operating income was $24.0 million, which included $3.6 million in net restructuring credits.
•Net income was $13.3 million, or $0.13 per diluted share, compared to $11.6 million, or $0.12 per diluted share last year. Adjusted net income(1) was $15.6 million, or $0.16 diluted adjusted earnings per share(1), compared to $20.7 million, or $0.21 diluted adjusted earnings per share(1) last year.
•Adjusted EBITDA(1) was $59.4 million, representing 5.0% of net sales.
Highlights for the 39 Weeks Ended October 3, 2026 as compared to the Prior Year Period:
•Net sales increased by 1.8% to $3.54 billion.
•Comparable store sales declined by 0.2%.
•Gross margin was 29.9% compared to 30.5% last year.
•Operating loss was $138.2 million, which included $158.0 million in non-cash goodwill impairment and $20.1 million in net restructuring charges.
•Net loss was $161.4 million, or $(1.63) per diluted share, compared to net loss of $6.8 million, or $(0.07) per diluted share last year. Adjusted net income(1) was $40.5 million, or $0.41 diluted adjusted earnings per share(1), compared to $56.5 million, or $0.57 diluted adjusted earnings per share(1) last year.
•Adjusted EBITDA(1) was $168.2 million, representing 4.8% of net sales.
“In the third quarter we returned to positive comparable store sales, driven by continued improvements to the execution of our opportunistic product offering,” said Jason Potter, President and CEO of Grocery Outlet. “Traffic accelerated and basket trends again improved sequentially as we enhanced our value proposition. In partnership with our operators and suppliers, we will continue to build on this progress with a focus on driving sustainable sales growth and improved profitability over time. This stronger foundation increases our confidence in our long-term opportunity.”

Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Financial Summary
Net sales increased 0.9% to $1.18 billion due to new store sales and a 0.5% increase in comparable store sales, partially offset by decreased sales from store closures as a result of the Optimization Plan as further discussed below. The increase in comparable store sales was driven by a 2.4% increase in the number of transactions, partially offset by a 1.9% decrease in average transaction size. We opened 14 new stores and closed zero stores, ending the quarter with 561 stores in 16 states.
Gross profit decreased slightly to $353.0 million. Gross margin was 30.0%, a decline of 40 basis points due primarily to the impact of product promotions to drive sales, higher transportation expenses, and the impact from a broad reduction in consumer demand for produce as a result of a Cyclospora outbreak associated with certain fresh produce items, partially offset by a favorable shift in sales mix toward opportunistic products.
Selling, general and administrative expenses increased slightly to $332.5 million. As a percentage of net sales, SG&A expenses declined 10 basis points to 28.2%.
Operating income was $24.0 million, which included $3.6 million in net restructuring credits related to the Optimization Plan consisting of $15.8 million in non-cash credits primarily from the net write-off of lease right-of-use assets and lease liabilities, partially offset by $12.2 million in cash charges.
39 Weeks Ended October 3, 2026 Financial Summary
Net sales increased 1.8% to $3.54 billion due to new store sales, partially offset by decreased sales from store closures as a result of the Optimization Plan and a 0.2% decrease in comparable store sales. The decrease in comparable store sales was driven by a 2.3% decrease in average transaction size, partially offset by a 2.1% increase in the number of transactions. We opened 31 new stores and closed 40 stores, including 36 stores as a result of the Optimization Plan.
Gross profit was relatively unchanged at $1.06 billion. Gross margin decreased 60 basis points to 29.9% during the first three quarters, driven primarily by the impact of product promotions to drive sales, inventory markdowns and write-offs associated with the store closures under the Optimization Plan, and higher transportation expenses, partially offset by improvements in inventory management.
Selling, general and administrative expenses increased by 2.0% versus last year to $1.02 billion. As a percentage of net sales, SG&A expenses were relatively unchanged at 28.8% during the first three quarters.
Operating loss was $138.2 million, which included a $158.0 million non-cash goodwill impairment charge during the first quarter of fiscal 2026 as a result of a decline in stock price, and $20.1 million in net restructuring charges related to the Optimization Plan consisting of $29.7 million in cash charges, partially offset by $9.6 million in net non-cash credits primarily from the net write-off of lease right-of-use assets and lease liabilities. The non-cash goodwill impairment charge does not impact future operations.
Cash Flow & Capital Spending:
•Net cash provided by operating activities during the third quarter of fiscal 2026 was $18.2 million compared with $17.3 million for the third quarter last year. The increase in operating cash flow was driven primarily by higher accrued and other liabilities due primarily to timing, partially offset by lower operating lease liabilities as a result of the Optimization Plan.

•Capital expenditures for the third quarter of fiscal 2026, before tenant improvement allowances, were $28.1 million, a decrease of $16.3 million from the third quarter of fiscal 2025 due to lower spending on current and future new store openings and prior year investments in new warehouses. Capital expenditures, net of tenant improvement allowances, for the third quarter this year, were $25.7 million compared with $39.4 million for the same period last year.
Optimization Plan:
As previously reported, we initiated a business optimization plan during the first quarter of fiscal 2026, intended to strengthen long-term profitability and cash flow generation, improve operational execution, optimize our existing store footprint and align with our disciplined new store growth strategy (the "Optimization Plan"). Under the Optimization Plan, we closed 36 financially underperforming stores ("Closure Stores") in March and April of 2026. We have negotiated, or intend to negotiate, the termination, sublease or assignment of the store leases for the Closure Stores. In addition, we have substantially completed the termination of operator agreements with independent operators for the Closure Stores and certain other store locations (the "Operator Agreement Terminations").
We estimate that we will incur total net restructuring charges under the Optimization Plan of between $22 million and $29 million. We increased our estimated range in the third quarter of fiscal 2026 primarily due to an update in expected lease termination costs and idle property costs. We now expect the operational actions and financial impacts of the Optimization Plan to be substantially completed by the fourth quarter of fiscal 2027.

Non-GAAP Financial Information:
In addition to reporting financial results in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States ("GAAP"), management and the Board of Directors use EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA margin, adjusted net income, adjusted earnings per share, and cash-on-cash return as supplemental key metrics to assess our financial performance, and net leverage as a supplemental metric to assess our liquidity. These non-GAAP financial measures are also frequently used by analysts, investors and other interested parties to evaluate the Company and other companies in our industry. Management believes it is useful to investors and analysts to evaluate these non-GAAP financial measures on the same basis as management uses to evaluate our operating results and liquidity. Management uses these non-GAAP financial performance measures to supplement GAAP financial measures of performance to evaluate the effectiveness of our business strategies, to make budgeting decisions and to compare our performance against that of other peer companies using similar measures. In addition, we use adjusted EBITDA and adjusted earnings per share to supplement GAAP financial measures of performance to evaluate performance in connection with compensation decisions. Management believes that excluding items from operating income (loss), net income (loss) and earnings (net loss) per diluted share that may not be indicative of, or are unrelated to, our core operating results, and that may vary in frequency or magnitude, enhances the comparability of our results and provides additional information for analyzing trends in our business. Management uses net leverage to evaluate our overall liquidity and financial flexibility to pursue operational strategies and to evaluate our capital structure, and our ability to service our long-term debt obligations.
Management defines EBITDA as net income (loss) before net interest expense, income taxes and depreciation and amortization expenses. Adjusted EBITDA represents EBITDA adjusted to exclude share-based compensation expense (benefit), asset impairment and gain or loss on disposition, acquisition and integration costs, restructuring and related charges, goodwill impairment and certain other expenses that may not be indicative of, or are unrelated to, our core operating results, and that may vary in frequency or magnitude. Adjusted EBITDA margin is calculated as adjusted EBITDA divided by net sales, expressed as a percentage. Adjusted net income represents net income (loss) adjusted for the previously mentioned adjusted EBITDA adjustments, further adjusted for the amortization of property and equipment purchase accounting asset step-ups and deferred financing costs, tax adjustment to normalize the effective tax rate, and tax effect of total adjustments. Basic adjusted earnings per share is calculated using adjusted net income, as defined above, and basic weighted-average shares outstanding. Diluted adjusted earnings per share is calculated using adjusted net income, as defined above, and diluted weighted-average shares outstanding. Management defines cash-on-cash returns as Four Wall EBITDA divided by total net cash investment. Four Wall EBITDA includes store level costs such as product and distribution costs, commissions, occupancy, marketing and other related costs. A definition of net leverage and a related reconciliation to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure can be found on the Investor Relations section of our website under "Financial Information—Quarterly Results."
These non-GAAP financial measures may not be comparable to similar measures reported by other companies and have limitations as analytical tools, and you should not consider them in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of our results as reported under GAAP. We address the limitations of the non-GAAP financial measures through the use of various GAAP measures. In the future we will incur expenses or charges such as those added back to calculate adjusted EBITDA or adjusted net income. The presentation of these non-GAAP financial measures should not be construed as an inference that future results will be unaffected by the adjustments used to derive such non-GAAP measures.

About Grocery Outlet:
Based in Emeryville, California, Grocery Outlet is a growth-oriented extreme value retailer of quality, name-brand consumables and fresh products sold primarily through a network of independently operated stores. Grocery Outlet and its subsidiaries have more than 560 stores in California, Washington, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Tennessee, Nevada, Idaho, Virginia, Maryland, North Carolina, Ohio, Georgia, New Jersey, Alabama, Delaware and Kentucky.
