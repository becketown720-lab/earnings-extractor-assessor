DeFi Development Corp. Reports Strong Q3 2026 Results; NAV per Share More Than Quadruples and SOL per Share Grows Over 20%
BOCA RATON, FL — November 12, 2026 — DeFi Development Corp. (Nasdaq: DFDV) (the “Company” or “DeFi Dev Corp.”), the first U.S. public company with a treasury strategy built to accumulate and compound Solana (“SOL”), today announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and released its quarterly shareholder letter.

Shareholder Letter Highlights
●SOL per share (“SPS”) increased more than 20% from August 12 through September 30. 2026.
●Net asset value (“NAV”) per share more than quadrupled over the same period, increasing over 300% from August 12 through September 30, 2026.
●Total SOL and SOL equivalents increased to 2,549,137 at September 30, up approximately 10% from the August 12 earnings update.
●SOL-denominated borrowings declined approximately 13%, from 562,000 SOL on August 12 to 487,000 SOL on September 30.
●Cash, cash equivalents, stablecoins, and marketable securities more than doubled from June 30 to September 30, strengthening the Company’s flexibility to fund operations and pursue treasury growth.
●Launched CHAD (Nasdaq: CHAD), establishing a perpetual source of capital intended to grow SOL holdings without issuing common shares, with no stated maturity or scheduled principal repayment.

Recent Developments
●CHAD first closed at its $10 stated amount (“par”) just 48 calendar days after its trading debut, faster than either Strive’s SATA or Strategy’s STRC, measured from trading debut to the first closing price at or above par.
●CHAD’s annual dividend rate increased to 14%, effective November 2, with daily cash dividends when declared. The Company is focused on broadening CHAD’s investor base, deepening liquidity, and scaling issuance on attractive terms.
●Total SOL and SOL equivalents reached 2,609,829 as of November 9, up approximately 13% from August 12, following nine consecutive weeks of SOL purchases.

About DeFi Development Corp.
DeFi Development Corp. (Nasdaq: DFDV) has adopted a treasury policy under which the principal holding in its treasury reserve is allocated to SOL. Through this strategy, the Company provides investors with direct economic exposure to SOL, while also actively participating in the growth of the Solana ecosystem. In addition to holding and staking SOL, DeFi Development Corp. operates its own validator infrastructure, generating staking rewards and fees from delegated stake. The Company is also engaged across decentralized finance (DeFi) opportunities and continues to explore innovative ways to support and benefit from Solana’s expanding application layer.
The Company is also an AI-powered online platform that connects the commercial real estate industry by providing value-add services and software subscriptions to multifamily and commercial property professionals, as the Company connects the increasingly complex ecosystem that stakeholders have to manage. The Company’s data and software offerings are generally offered on a subscription basis as software as a service.

SPS and NAV per share are supplemental, management-defined analytical measures used to assess the Company’s treasury strategy and net SOL exposure attributable to common shareholders. The growth comparisons cover August 12 through September 30, 2026. SPS growth uses a constant SOL price of $117.99 and values both convertible notes at outstanding principal. NAV per share growth uses SOL prices of $75.53 and $117.99 on the respective dates and values the July 2030 convertible notes at 64.0% and 66.5% of outstanding principal, respectively; the April 2030 notes remain valued at principal. NAV includes the value of SOL and SOL equivalents, cash, stablecoins, and marketable securities, less SOL-denominated borrowings, convertible debt valued under the applicable methodology, other debt, and preferred equity at its aggregate stated amount. Both comparisons use adjusted common shares outstanding, including shares underlying restricted stock units and vested options, without assuming conversion of convertible debt or including incremental warrant shares. These measures do not represent GAAP book value, liquidation proceeds, or shareholder investment returns. Additional methodology and limitations appear in the shareholder letter.
