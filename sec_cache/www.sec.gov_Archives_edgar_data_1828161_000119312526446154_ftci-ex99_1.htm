FTC Solar Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Third Quarter Highlights and Recent Developments

 

 

•
Third quarter revenue up 25% q/q; profitability metrics within, or better than, target ranges
•
Significant new project wins across multiple geographies with several new customers, including Tata Power Solar and Evren, platform backed by Brookfield
•
Targeting 46% revenue growth in fourth quarter at mid-point
•
Expect continued strong growth and Adjusted EBITDA profitability in 2027 on strength of pipeline

 

AUSTIN, Texas — November 4, 2026– FTC Solar, Inc. (Nasdaq: FTCI), a leading provider of solar tracker systems, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“We delivered a strong quarter with revenue up 25% sequentially and margins ahead of our targets,” said Anthony Carroll, CEO of FTC Solar. “We expect continued significant growth in the fourth quarter with a path to EBITDA profitability in the first quarter of next year. With significant new awards and growing momentum across North America, India, Australia, and Europe and with AI and robotics enhancing our current and future productivity, we’re well positioned for a strong 2027. Additionally, we obtained independent validation from DNV that our tracker installs in an industry-leading 81 labor hours per megawatt, confirming what customers are telling us: speed in the field is a winning competitive advantage.”

 

Third Quarter Results

Total third-quarter revenue was $32.8 million. This represents an increase of 25.4% compared to the prior quarter revenue and an increase of 26.1% compared to the year-ago quarter.

 

GAAP gross profit was $1.9 million, or 5.8% of revenue, compared to gross loss of $2.2 million, or 8.5% of revenue, in the prior quarter. Non-GAAP gross profit was $2.8 million or 8.4% of revenue. This compares to Non-GAAP gross profit of $2.0 million in the prior-year period.

Subsequent Events

Subsequent to quarter end, the company received a number of new project awards, contracts and other agreements across various geographies. A few highlights include:

 

North America

•
Contracted 750-megawatt (MW) project from a well-regarded developer with a high-profile backer for a project to begin in 2027 and continue into 2028;
•
Awarded 175MW project in U.S. from a new customer;
•
The company is working with Linxon and private equity firm Energy Transition Capital to advance a portfolio of solar projects across multiple locations in Puerto Rico. FTC is currently providing engineering, design and qualification support for this roughly 600-megawatt portfolio; This is among the first activity under the recently announced collaboration with Linxon and Hitatchi Energy establishing a strategic collaboration framework to jointly pursue and deliver utility scale solar projects across North America and other markets;

 

Europe

•
Announced a new collaboration and Master Supply Agreement with AGR Renewables. Under the terms of the agreement, FTC Solar will provide solar trackers, along with design, engineering, site assessment, and software solutions, as needed, to enhance installation speed, ensure site quality and reliability, and improve project profitability. The companies have established an initial target of 500 MW of projects in the United Kingdom through 2028, with the flexibility to significantly expand target volumes and extend the collaboration into additional geographies. An initial 30 megawatts under this agreement has been contracted;

 

India

•
Contracted a 118 MW project with Tata Power Solar, the solar subsidiary of India’s largest power and energy company. The project is located in the Gadag district of Karnataka, India. Initial production for the project is already underway;
•
Contracted a 121 MW project with Evren, the Indian renewable development platform backed by Brookfield. The project is located in Andhra Pradesh, India. Initial production for the project is expected to begin in, or before, the first quarter of 2027;

 

Australia

•
Signed an MOU with six significant materials suppliers in Australia to further strengthen local content capabilities and support the company’s growth in the region.

 

Outlook

The company expects fourth quarter revenue to grow by approximately 46% relative to the third quarter, based on the midpoint of the guidance range. The company expects to see continued sequential revenue growth in the first

 

 

quarter of 2027 and to achieve profitability on an Adjusted EBITDA basis. In addition, the company expects to see significant revenue growth in 2027, with full-year profitability.

About FTC Solar Inc.

Founded in 2017 by a group of renewable energy industry veterans, FTC Solar is a global provider of solar tracker systems, technology, software, and engineering services. Solar trackers significantly increase energy production at solar power installations by dynamically optimizing solar panel orientation to the sun. FTC Solar’s innovative tracker designs provide compelling performance and reliability, with an industry-leading installation cost-per-watt advantage.

Notes to Reconciliations of Non-GAAP Financial Measures to Nearest Comparable GAAP Measures

We utilize Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Net Loss, and Adjusted EPS as supplemental measures of our performance. We define Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) plus (i) provision for (benefit from) income taxes, (ii) interest expense, less interest income, (iii) depreciation expense, (iv) amortization expense, (v) stock-based compensation, (vi) loss from changes in the fair value of our warrant liability, and (vii) Chief Executive Officer ("CEO") transition costs, costs associated with our reverse stock split, severance and certain other costs (credits). We also deduct (i) the contingent gains arising from earnout payments and project escrow releases relating to the disposal of our investment in an unconsolidated subsidiary, and (ii) gains from changes in the fair value of our warrant liability from net income or loss in arriving at Adjusted EBITDA. We define Adjusted Net Loss as net income (loss) plus (i) amortization of debt discount and issue costs and intangibles, (ii) stock-based compensation, (iii) loss from changes in the fair value of our warrant liability, (iv) CEO transition costs, costs associated with our reverse stock split, severance and certain other costs (credits), and (v) the income tax expense (benefit) of those adjustments, if any. We also deduct (i) the contingent gains arising from earnout payments and project escrow releases relating to the disposal of our investment in an unconsolidated subsidiary, and (ii) gains from changes in the fair value of our warrant liability from net income (loss) in arriving at Adjusted Net Loss. Adjusted EPS is defined as Adjusted Net Loss on a per share basis using our weighted average diluted shares outstanding.

 

Non-GAAP gross profit (loss), Non-GAAP operating expense, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Net Loss and Adjusted EPS are intended as supplemental measures of performance that are neither required by, nor presented in accordance with, U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”). We present these Non-GAAP measures, many of which are commonly used by investors and analysts, because we believe they assist those investors and analysts in comparing our performance across reporting periods on an ongoing basis by excluding items that we do not believe are indicative of our core operating performance. In addition, we use Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Net Loss and Adjusted EPS to evaluate the effectiveness of our business strategies.

 

Non-GAAP gross profit (loss), Non-GAAP operating expense, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Net Loss and Adjusted EPS should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for performance measures calculated in accordance with GAAP, and you should not rely on any single financial measure to evaluate our business. These Non-GAAP financial measures, when presented, are reconciled to the most closely applicable GAAP measure as disclosed below.
