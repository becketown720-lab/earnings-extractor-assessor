Gemini Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

Total revenue of $40.9 million, with services revenue and interest income at 59% of net revenue as platform spot trading volume declined 73% year-over-year; total operating expenses decreased 30% year-over-year

NEW YORK, November 12, 2026 – Gemini Space Station, Inc. (“Gemini,” the “Company,” “we,” or “us”) (NASDAQ: GEMI), a crypto and markets company, today announced financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Total revenue was $40.9 million, compared to $50.6 million in the third quarter of 2025, a decrease of 19% year-over-year. Exchange revenue declined 47% year-over-year to $13.4 million, as total trading volume on our platform fell to $4.5 billion from $16.4 billion in the prior-year period. Services revenue and interest income was $23.3 million, compared to $23.4 million in Q3 2025, and represented 59% of net revenue.

Total operating expenses decreased 30% year-over-year to $119.9 million from $171.4 million, and declined 2% sequentially from $122.4 million in the second quarter of 2026, primarily driven by lower compensation costs and reduced marketing spend, reflecting continued cost discipline and lower headcount. Net loss improved 63% year-over-year to $58.6 million from $159.5 million.

“Operating expenses declined for a third consecutive quarter, and as we invest in growth, we intend to stay disciplined about where those dollars go,” said Tyler Winklevoss, CEO of Gemini. “We’re clear-eyed about the work ahead and committed to getting it right: deepening our relationships with the customers who use Gemini, continuing to iterate, and investing where customers and markets are headed. We expect to be judged on results, and we intend to earn the confidence of our customers and shareholders one quarter at a time.”

“We started Gemini in 2014 because we believed crypto would change how people save, spend and move value, and we’re in this for the long term,” said Cameron Winklevoss, President of Gemini. “Gemini 2.0 is our bet that the platforms that last will be the ones that own the regulated infrastructure underneath. We’re building ours to serve customers directly and to be something other platforms can build on.”

Third Quarter Results:

●Total revenue decreased 19% year-over-year and 10% sequentially to $40.9 million. Net revenue was $39.2 million, compared with $49.8 million in Q3 2025 and $43.7 million in Q2 2026.
●Transaction revenue decreased 40% year-over-year and 10% sequentially to $15.9 million from $26.3 million in Q3 2025, driven by a significant decline in crypto trading activity.
●Exchange revenue decreased 47% year-over-year to $13.4 million, primarily reflecting lower retail trading activity, partially offset by higher institutional revenue. Sequentially, exchange revenue increased 7%, while spot trading volume increased 18% to $4.5 billion, supported by increased institutional Zcash activity and expanded affiliated market-making.
●Total trading volume was $4.5 billion, compared with $16.4 billion in Q3 2025 and $3.8 billion in Q2 2026. Approximately $1.2 billion, or 26%, of quarterly


spot volume involved transactions between external customers and our market maker.
●OTC revenue increased 60% year-over-year and decreased 64% sequentially to $1.7 million, compared with $1.1 million in Q3 2025 and $4.7 million in Q2 2026. The year-over-year increase reflects growth in electronic OTC (eOTC) client activity; the sequential decline is consistent with the variable nature of large institutional trades.
●Prediction markets revenue was $0.8 million, up 52% from $0.5 million in Q2 2026. Prediction markets revenue is reported net of maker and taker incentive rebates.
●Services revenue increased 7% year-over-year and decreased 10% sequentially to $21.3 million. Services revenue and interest income together totaled $23.3 million, compared to $23.4 million in Q3 2025, and represented 59% of net revenue, consistent with the second quarter and up from 47% in the prior-year quarter.
●Credit card revenue increased 82% year-over-year, driven by growth in the Gemini Credit Card portfolio, and decreased 4% sequentially to $15.5 million.
●Staking revenue decreased 29% year-over-year to $4.2 million, primarily driven by lower average staked asset balances and lower realized staking yields. Sequentially, staking revenue increased 3% reflecting price appreciation in underlying staked assets.
●Advisory fee revenue was $0.6 million, compared with $2.7 million in the second quarter, reflecting completion in July of the recognition period for the compensatory warrant arrangement associated with an advisory services agreement with a strategic customer.
●Interest income was $2.0 million, compared with $3.5 million in Q3 2025 and $2.4 million in Q2 2026, reflecting lower average customer custodial balances, partially offset by margin lending fees from our spot margin trading offering, which had no comparable activity in the prior-year period.
●Total operating expenses decreased 30% year-over-year and 2% sequentially to $119.9 million, marking the third consecutive quarterly decline. The year-over-year decrease was driven primarily by lower sales and marketing expenses and lower salaries and compensation, partially offset by higher transaction losses.
●Salaries and compensation decreased 32% year-over-year and increased 17% sequentially to $56.4 million, including $32.7 million of stock-based compensation, compared with $20.3 million in the second quarter. The sequential increase in stock-based compensation reflected equity-based annual bonus awards to Company employees recognized in July. Excluding stock-based compensation, salaries and compensation decreased 36% year-over-year and 15% sequentially to $23.7 million, reflecting the benefits of the February 2026 reduction in force.
●Sales and marketing decreased 75% year-over-year and 6% sequentially to $8.3 million, reflecting a more selective approach to marketing investments. Within sales and marketing:
●Marketing acquisition, brand spend and other costs decreased 97% year-over-year to $0.7 million.
●Credit card rewards and promotional and referral incentives decreased 41% year-over-year to $7.6 million, as lower promotional and referral incentives more than offset a 21% increase in credit card rewards.


●Transaction losses increased 38% year-over-year and declined from $20.1 million in the second quarter to $10.6 million, including credit card provision expense of $9.2 million, compared with $2.8 million in Q3 2025 and $16.1 million in Q2 2026. Credit card charge-offs increased to $11.5 million from $3.4 million, primarily reflecting accelerated charge-offs of accounts associated with the previously disclosed first-quarter fraud event. Managed credit card receivables were $202.2 million at quarter-end, compared with $150.6 million a year ago.
●Non-fraud 30-plus-day delinquency increased to 3.4% from 3.3% in the second quarter. The Company believes this reflects, in part, the seasoning of accounts added during the rapid portfolio growth in the second half of 2025. During that expansion, receivables grew faster than delinquent balances developed, reducing the reported delinquency rate. As those accounts mature and receivables decline, both factors can contribute to a higher delinquency rate.
●Technology expenses were $19.6 million, down 4% year-over-year, primarily reflecting lower amortization and impairment expenses. Expenses increased from $18.8 million in the second quarter, primarily due to higher cloud computing and infrastructure costs.
●General and administrative expenses, including legal expenses were $19.8 million, up 2% year-over-year, as higher legal expenses were largely offset by lower professional services costs. Expenses decreased from $20.6 million in the second quarter.
●Transaction processing decreased 39% year-over-year to $5.3 million, primarily driven by lower staking rewards and banking and compliance fees, partially offset by higher processing costs supporting new and expanded products, including Gemini Predictions.
●Operating loss was $79.0 million, compared with $120.8 million in Q3 2025 and $76.9 million in Q2 2026. Sequentially, lower revenue and a $12.4 million increase in stock-based compensation more than offset a $14.9 million reduction in all other operating expenses. Adjusted Operating Loss, a non-GAAP measure, narrowed 42% year-over-year and 21% sequentially to $39.0 million, its fourth consecutive quarter of improvement.
●Net loss narrowed 63% year-over-year and 46% sequentially to $58.6 million, compared to $159.5 million in Q3 2025.
●Net loss per share, basic and diluted, was $0.46, compared with $6.67 in the prior-year quarter.
●Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure, was a loss of $11.6 million, compared with losses of $52.4 million in Q3 2025 and $74.0 million in Q2 2026. The sequential improvement reflected lower credit card provision and employee compensation costs, together with a $50.8 million favorable swing in net realized and unrealized results on crypto assets and related-party crypto loans, from a $25.0 million loss in Q2 to a $25.7 million gain in Q3.
●Cash and cash equivalents totaled $194.1 million as of September 30, 2026, compared with $252.2 million as of December 31, 2025.
●Monthly Transacting Users were 577,000, down 2% year-over-year and 1% sequentially.
●Assets on Platform were $13.4 billion as of September 30, 2026, compared with $21.3 billion as of September 30, 2025 and $8.4 billion as of June 30, 2026. The year-over-year


decrease reflects lower crypto asset valuations relative to the prior-year period and select institutional custody net outflows.

Business Highlights
Delivering on operational discipline
●Total operating expenses down 30% year-over-year to $119.9 million, the third consecutive sequential decline. Operating loss improved 35% year-over-year to $79.0 million. Adjusted Operating Loss narrowed 42% year-over-year to $39.0 million, the fourth consecutive quarterly improvement.
Gemini Is Opening Its Regulated Prediction Markets Infrastructure to Distribution and Clearing Partners
●On August 24, 2026, Gemini and Apex Fintech Solutions announced a non-binding letter of intent under which Gemini Titan, LLC would become the exclusive regulated venue for crypto event contracts distributed through Apex's Futures Commission Merchant (FCM) to Apex's brokerage customers. The letter of intent also allows the parties the flexibility to collaborate on additional contract categories, including sports, economics, and financial markets, on a non-exclusive basis. The letter of intent is subject to negotiation of definitive documentation, including with respect to the full scope of the implementation and integration of the intended relationship.
●Expanding distribution is a core part of Gemini’s prediction markets strategy. Gemini intends to make its in-house exchange and clearinghouse available to brokerages, FCMs, and other designated contract markets that want to offer event contracts without building their own infrastructure, and is in active discussions with additional prospective distribution and clearing partners.
Gemini Expands Access to Its Platform for Developers and AI Agents
●More customers now use AI tools to research markets and get context before they invest. Gemini’s goal is to let customers bring the AI agent of their choice to a single Gemini account spanning crypto, stocks, and prediction markets, and to connect to new AI platforms as they emerge.
●In October, Gemini made its Model Context Protocol (MCP) server available on AI platforms, including Anthropic and OpenAI. Customers can use AI agents to pull real-time Gemini market data and research context directly within those tools.
●Gemini rebuilt its developer platform with dedicated documentation for each product line and released a public, dependency-free TypeScript SDK covering its trading products.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Adjusted EBITDA

Management believes that Adjusted EBITDA, which is a measure not presented in accordance with GAAP, provides investors with additional useful information in evaluating our performance. We use this non-GAAP measure internally to evaluate performance and to make financial, investment and operational decisions. We believe that presentation of this non-GAAP measure provides investors with greater transparency with respect to our operating results and that this measure is useful for period-to-period comparisons of results. Management also believes that providing this non-GAAP measure helps investors evaluate the Company’s operating performance, profitability and business trends in a way that is consistent with how management evaluates such matters.

We define Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss), adjusted to exclude provision for (benefit from) income taxes, interest expense, depreciation and amortization, stock-based compensation expense, impairment, restructuring charges, non-recurring legal contingencies, settlement and related costs, change in fair value on related party convertible notes, change in fair value on related party loans, gain on conversion of convertible notes and term loans, and unrealized foreign exchange loss (gain). Among other non-cash and non-recurring items, Adjusted EBITDA excludes stock-based compensation expense, which has recently been, and will continue to be for the foreseeable future, a significant recurring expense for our business and an important part of our compensation strategy. In addition, on February 5, 2026, the Company announced its plans to wind down operations in the United Kingdom, European Union, other European jurisdictions, and Australia. As such, beginning with the quarter ended March 31, 2026, Adjusted EBITDA also excludes related restructuring charges, which primarily relate to workforce reductions, lease exit costs, and other actions taken to streamline our operations and that we believe are unusual in nature and/or infrequent in occurrence and are not indicative of our ongoing operating activities.

Adjusted Operating Loss

Management believes that Adjusted Operating Loss, which is a measure not presented in accordance with GAAP, may provide investors with additional useful information in evaluating our operating performance and operating leverage. We use this non-GAAP measure to evaluate the efficiency of our cost structure, the relationship between revenue growth and operating expenses, and the progress of our ongoing operations. We believe that presentation of this non-GAAP measure provides investors with greater transparency with respect to our operating results by removing the effects of certain non-cash items and other adjustments that management believes may obscure our underlying operational performance, and that this measure is useful for period-to-period comparisons of results.

We define Adjusted Operating Loss as operating loss, adjusted to exclude depreciation and amortization, stock-based compensation, impairment, restructuring charges, and non-recurring legal contingencies, settlement and related costs.



Other companies, including companies in our industry, may calculate similarly titled non-GAAP measures differently or may use other non-GAAP measures to evaluate their performance, all of which could reduce the usefulness of our non-GAAP financial measures as a tool for comparison. A reconciliation of these non-GAAP measures is provided in this earnings release to the most directly comparable financial measure stated in accordance with GAAP. Investors are encouraged to review the related GAAP financial measures and the reconciliation of non-GAAP financial measures to their most directly comparable GAAP financial measures, and not to rely on any single financial measure to evaluate our business. These non-GAAP financial measures are presented for supplemental informational purposes only, have limitations as an analytical tool, and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for financial information presented in accordance with GAAP.

Key Performance Metrics

In addition to the measures presented in our unaudited condensed consolidated financial statements, management uses key performance metrics to help evaluate our business, identify trends affecting our business, formulate business plans, and make strategic decisions. Our key performance metrics include MTUs, LTUs, Card Sign-Ups, Trading Volume, and Assets on Platform. Definitions of these key performance metrics can be found below:

●Monthly Transacting User: any retail or institutional user who has engaged in any revenue-generating activity or whose account otherwise generated revenue for the Company in the trailing thirty days. MTUs presented for a quarter represent the MTUs as of the last day of the respective quarter. MTUs presented as of the end of a year represent the MTUs as of the last day of that year.
●Lifetime Transacting User: LTUs represent the cumulative number of unique MTUs who have ever transacted on our platform and continue to maintain an open account, measured since inception.
●Card Sign-Ups: the cumulative number of approved applications for the Gemini Credit Card in the relevant period. Card Sign-Ups include customers who have been approved to open an account, regardless of whether they have subsequently activated or used their card or whether the account later remains open.
●Trading Volume: the total U.S. dollar equivalent value of spot matched trades transacted between a buyer and seller through our platform during the period of measurement, including trades in which our affiliated market maker is a counterparty.
●Assets on Platform: the total value of assets held on our platform and includes digital assets in custody, staking, and exchange products, user custodial fiat, and GUSD reserve assets.
For more information and a more detailed discussion of our Key Performance Metrics, refer to our Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 that has been filed with the SEC.
