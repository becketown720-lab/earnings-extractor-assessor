Data I/O Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

​

October Transformational Acquisition Doubles Revenues and Expands into Diverse High-Growth Markets

​

Redmond, WA – November 12, 2026 -- Data I/O Corporation (NASDAQ: DAIO), the leading global provider of data programming equipment, provisioning services and security provisioning for Flash-based microcontrollers, and high-density memory devices, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

​

Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Highlights

​

●Revenues of $4.9 million with bookings of $5.0 million
●Gross margin as a percentage of sales was 58.0%, as compared to 57.0% in Q2
●EBITDA adjusted for equity compensation and one-time expenses improved sequentially and versus prior year period*
●Restructurings and expense optimizations support breakeven adjusted EBITDA for the quarter
●Cash on September 30, 2026 was $9.4 million as compared to $10.8 million on June 30, 2026
●Secured $13.25 million direct investment and $7.5 million bank line of credit in October 2026
●Closed on transformational acquisition of V-TEK, Inc. and A&J Programming, Inc. (collectively, “V-TEK”)
●Expansion into services and high-growth end markets including data center, AI, photonics, industrial automation, and robotics

​

* See EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation in schedules following this release.

Management Comments

​

Commenting on the financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 and recent developments, William Wentworth, President and CEO of Data I/O Corporation, said, “The most important measure of our recent performance and our prospects for the future is the completion of the transformational acquisition of V-TEK.

​

“As Data I/O’s first acquisition in decades, we’re proud to have completed the transaction and the required recapitalization following several years of a cyclical downturn in automotive. Total consideration was $23 million, with $3 million paid in Data I/O common stock. This displays the conviction of bringing these two companies together and the future value of the combined global business.

​

“The acquisition is anticipated to double revenues and be accretive to earnings. The integration plan has kicked off across multiple functional departments. Over the last two years we have used AI to optimize the business, and we will apply AI to the integration and ongoing operations at V-TEK. We will follow our mandate of best people and best practices.

​

“The NEW Data I/O has become a reality. With the V-TEK acquisition, our expanded products and services now address multibillion-dollar markets. The NEW Data I/O serves industries such as data centers, AI, photonics, industrial automation, robotics, medical, military, aerospace and defense, significantly reducing our reliance on automotive.

​

“We are shifting our mix towards software and services, diversifying our revenue streams and reducing our exposure to the capital equipment cycle. At the same time, we continue to invest in our programming platform, including new automation, programming technology and security provisioning. V-TEK’s existing programming business, A&J Programming, accelerates our strategic plan to provide Programming-as-a-Service.

​

“Data I/O will remain focused on optimizing the business, using technology and realigning costs to our new go-to-market strategy, and we continue to review expense reductions that will improve profitability. At the same time, we are investing in our core business to strengthen our capital equipment business and support our growing programming services business. The consolidation of V-TEK expands our ability to scale, enter new markets, increase operating leverage, and generate cash.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

​

Net sales in the third quarter 2026 were $4.9 million, as compared with $5.2 million in the second quarter 2026 and $5.4 million in the prior year period. Sales in the current quarter reflect continued revenue recovery and stable bookings in the platform business offset by timing-related softness in adapters. Demand for platform equipment appears to have inflected over the past two quarters following a prolonged downward cycle that had been negatively impacted by a realignment of technology spending, with AI-related investments at the forefront and a reassessment of EV capacity and related automotive manufacturing.

​

Third quarter 2026 bookings were $5.0 million, as compared with $4.9 million in the second quarter 2026. Bookings through the first six months of the year are indicative of the current market for programmable devices which has been accelerating within end markets not traditionally targeted by the Company, including edge AI and robotics. Bookings within the automotive electronics market was favorable for platform sales during the second quarter but softer for consumable adapter sales.

​

For the third quarter 2026, consumable adapters and services represented 48% of total revenue, providing a stable base of re-occurring revenue. This compares with 55% in the second quarter and 81% in the first quarter when total sales were lower. Platform sales represented 52% of total revenue in the third quarter, up from 45% in the second quarter and 19% in the first quarter. Backlog on September 30, 2026 was $1.5 million, down from $2.1 million at June 30, 2026 and $2.6 million at March 31, 2026. Deferred revenue was $0.9 million on September 30, 2026, as compared to $1.1 million at June 30, 2026 and $1.5 million at March 31, 2026.

​

Gross margin as a percentage of sales was 58.0% in the third quarter 2026, as compared to 57.0% in the second quarter 2026 and 50.7% in the third quarter 2025. The increased gross margin reflects the sale of several lower-cost units during the quarter, an increased focus on value-based pricing, and incremental efficiencies in operations. Aside from the low-cost platforms sold in the third quarter, direct material costs improved slightly compared to prior periods as the Company continued actively to mitigate the impact of tariffs and other inflationary pressures.

​

Operating expenses for the third quarter 2026 were $4.9 million, which included approximately $1.5 million in one-time expenses primarily related to debt and financing expenses, the IT/ERP transition, non-cash inventory reserve adjustments and other one-time charges. This compares to operating expenses of $4.1 million in the third quarter 2025 which included approximately $0.6 million in one-time expenses related to the investigation and remediation of a cybersecurity incident, the Company’s leadership transition as well as investments in the core programming platform and information systems.*

​

Operating loss in the third quarter 2026 was ($2.0) million on revenues of $4.9 million compared to the loss of ($1.4) million on revenues of $5.4 million in the third quarter of 2025.

​

Net loss in the third quarter 2026 was ($2.7) million or ($0.27) per share, compared to net loss of ($1.4) million or ($0.15) per share in the third quarter 2025. The increased loss reflects higher operating expenses due to one-time items, partially offset by higher gross margins.

​

Adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization (“Adjusted EBITDA”), which excludes equity compensation and one-time expenses, was ($71,000) in the third quarter 2026, compared to ($563,000) in the third quarter 2025. Third quarter 2026 Adjusted EBITDA includes $1.4 million of one-time expenses, as compared to approximately $585,000 of one-time expenses in the prior year period.*

​

The Company’s cash balance remained strong at the end of the third quarter 2026 with $9.4 million as compared to $10.8 million on June 30, 2026. Data I/O’s net working capital increased to $14.7 million on September 30, 2026 from $10.8 million on June 30, 2026 and $12.3 million on December 31, 2025. The Company had no debt on September 30, 2026 after converting $6.2 million of convertible debt into preferred shares following the June 2026 private placement. These debentures had been classified as short-term debt and included in the balance sheet and for the working capital calculation at June 30, 2026. Subsequent to the end of the third quarter, the Company completed a private placement of securities for approximately $13.25 million in gross proceeds.

About Data I/O Corporation

​

Since 1972, Data I/O Corporation (NASDAQ: DAIO) has been the global leader in device programming. In 2026, Data I/O united three industry leaders with the transformational acquisition of V-TEK, Inc. and A&J Programming, Inc. The combination enables Data I/O to provide the world’s most comprehensive solutions to support the entire data provisioning supply chain.  These solutions include data programming and security provisioning, semiconductor handling automation for test, inspection and packaging, and Programming-as-a-Service. The Company serves customers in the automotive, industrial automation, data center, AI, photonics, medical, military, aerospace and defense, IoT, and consumer electronics markets. OEMs and manufacturers of any size can program, securely provision, test and package devices from early samples through high-volume production, using modular and customized systems and flexible service options. Data I/O’s solutions are backed by a portfolio of patents (dataio.com/Company/Patents) and a global network of distribution partners and support and service professionals, ensuring success for customers.

​

Learn more at www.dataio.com, www.vtekusa.com, and www.ajprogram.com.
