NEW JERSEY RESOURCES REPORTS FISCAL 2026
FOURTH-QUARTER AND YEAR-END RESULTS

WALL, N.J., November 18, 2026 — New Jersey Resources Corporation (NYSE: NJR) today reported financial and operating results for its fiscal 2026 fourth quarter and year-ended September 30, 2026.

Financial Highlights
Fiscal Year
•
Fiscal 2026 consolidated net income totaled $368.4 million, or $3.65 per share, compared with $335.6 million, or $3.35 per share, in fiscal 2025
•
Fiscal 2026 consolidated net financial earnings (NFE), a non-GAAP financial measure, totaled $361.6 million, or $3.58 per share, compared with $329.6 million, or $3.29 per share, in fiscal 2025
Quarterly
•
Fiscal 2026 fourth-quarter consolidated net income of $17.3 million, or $0.17 per share, compared with net income of $15.1 million, or $0.15 per share, in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025
•
Fiscal 2026 fourth-quarter consolidated NFE of $10.7 million, or $0.11 per share, compared with $16.2 million, or $0.16 per share, in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025

Operating Highlights
•
New Jersey Natural Gas (NJNG): Reduced the average customer bill by 9.0%, or approximately $158 annually, ahead of the 2026-2027 winter heating season, while continuing to invest in safe, reliable service

◦ Further supported customer affordability through a record $101 million investment in SAVEGREEN energy efficiency programs, reducing customers energy use and bills

◦
Through energy assistance outreach, NJNG connected customers with approximately $21 million in bill payment assistance
•
Clean Energy Ventures (CEV): Placed 14 commercial solar projects into service during fiscal 2026, adding 72.4 megawatts (MW)* of capacity and bringing total in-service commercial solar capacity to approximately 552 MW
•
Storage and Transportation (S&T):

◦
Adelphia Gateway (Adelphia): Received approval of its rate case settlement in November 2025, supporting higher operating income in fiscal 2026

◦
Leaf River Energy Center: (Leaf River): Received FERC authorization to expand its natural gas storage capacity to 55.2 Bcf
•
Energy Services (ES): Capitalized on winter-period natural gas price volatility, contributing to two net financial earnings per share (NFEPS) guidance range increases in fiscal 2026
•
Home Services: Named a Ruud Top Twenty Pro Partner Contractor for the 10th consecutive year

Fiscal 2027 Outlook
•
Maintains 7 to 9 percent long-term NFEPS growth target, starting from a fiscal 2025 base of $2.83 per share**
•
Introduces fiscal 2027 NFEPS guidance range of $3.25 to $3.40

* All MWs noted in DC
** The fiscal 2025 NFEPS base of $2.83 represents the midpoint of Fiscal 2025 NFEPS guidance, excluding the net impact of the sale of our residential portfolio

New Jersey Natural Gas (NJNG)
NJNG reported fiscal 2026 fourth-quarter net financial loss of $(24.4) million, compared with $(8.0) million during the same period in fiscal 2025. The decrease in NFE for the period was driven primarily by higher depreciation expense as a result of additional utility plant being placed into service and higher interest expense, partially offset by higher utility gross margin.

Fiscal 2026 NFE totaled $214.0 million, compared with NFE of $213.5 million in fiscal 2025. The  increase is due primarily to the full-year impact of base rates that became effective November 21, 2024, compared with  the partial-year impact recognized in fiscal 2025, along with customer growth and SAVEGREEN investments, partially offset by higher depreciation expense from additional plant placed in service.

Customers:

•
At September 30, 2026, NJNG serviced approximately 596,000 customers in New Jersey’s Monmouth, Ocean, Morris, Middlesex, Sussex and Burlington counties, compared to approximately 589,000 customers as of September 30, 2025.

Regulatory Filings:

•
On June 1, 2026, NJNG submitted its annual Basic Gas Supply Service (BGSS), Conservation Incentive Program (CIP) and energy-efficiency filings to the New Jersey Board of Public Utilities (BPU). Taken together, the filings were designed to reduce the average residential customer bill by 9.0%, or approximately $158 annually, ahead of the 2026–2027 winter season. The proposed rate changes were subsequently approved on a provision basis by the BPU and became effective October 1, 2026.
•
Also, on June 1, 2026, NJNG filed a base rate case with the BPU, seeking a $157.6 million increase to its base rates. The filing is based on an overall rate of return on rate base of 7.60 percent with a return on common equity of 10.10 percent. The proposed increase reflects a 55.50 percent common equity component. On September 21, 2026, the filing was updated to reflect actual results through July 31, 2026, which modified the requested increase to approximately $158.3 million and changed the Company’s overall rate of return on rate base to 7.52 percent.
•
Once all filings are implemented, NJNG anticipates that the overall net result will leave NJNG annualized average customer bills nearly flat. Unless otherwise noted, NJNG cannot predict the outcome or ultimate resolution for open regulatory matters.
•
On September 30, 2026, NJNG filed a letter petition asking the BPU to approve Program Year 7 (PY7), which would continue its SAVEGREEN program through June 30, 2028. The filing has two options: a $127.0 million program based on the BPU's PY7 Framework and a $125.5 million alternative proposed by NJNG. Each option would raise annual recoveries by approximately $17.0 million starting July 1, 2027, if approved.

BGSS Incentive Programs1:

•
BGSS incentive programs contributed $22.8 million to utility gross margin during fiscal 2026, compared with $18.4 million for the same period in fiscal 2025. This increase was primarily driven by increased margins from off-system sales and capacity release due to market volatility as a result of colder than normal weather.
1 BGSS incentive savings represent value created through supply and capacity optimization and shared with customers through the BGSS clause.

For more information on utility gross margin, please see "Non-GAAP Financial Information" below.

Energy-Efficiency Programs:

•
SAVEGREEN® invested $101.0 million in fiscal 2026 in energy-efficiency upgrades for customers' homes and businesses. Investments in SAVEGREEN® are incremental to rate base and earn near-real time returns through an annual recovery mechanism.

Clean Energy Ventures (CEV)
CEV reported fiscal 2026 fourth-quarter NFE of $33.5 million, compared with $23.8 million during the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025, reflecting higher Renewable Energy Credits and electricity revenue, partially offset by higher depreciation and interest expense associated with capital invested over the past year.

Fiscal 2026 NFE totaled $37.6 million, compared with NFE of $61.2 million in fiscal 2025. The decrease was primarily due to a gain from the sale of CEV's residential solar portfolio assets that was recognized in the prior year period.

Solar Investment Update:

 •
During fiscal 2026, CEV placed fourteen commercial projects into service, adding 72.4 MW* to installed capacity.

•
As of September 30, 2026, CEV had approximately 552 MW of commercial solar capacity in service across New Jersey, New York, Connecticut, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, Indiana, Michigan and Maine.

* All MWs noted in DC

Storage and Transportation (S&T)
S&T reported fiscal 2026 fourth-quarter NFE of $9.6 million, compared with $4.6 million during the same period in fiscal 2025. Fiscal 2026 NFE totaled $33.4 million, compared with NFE of $18.5 million in fiscal 2025.

NFE increased during both periods mainly due to higher operating income at Adelphia Gateway primarily due to the impact of its rate case settled on November 4, 2025 and higher firm storage rates at Leaf River.

Energy Services (ES)
ES reported fiscal 2026 fourth-quarter net financial loss of $(7.9) million, compared with net financial loss of $(4.5) million for the same period in fiscal 2025.

Fiscal 2026 NFE totaled $76.7 million, compared with NFE of $34.9 million for the same period in fiscal 2025. The increase in NFE was primarily due to higher natural gas price volatility that allowed ES to capture additional financial margin.

Home Services and Other Operations
Home Services and Other Operations reported fiscal 2026 fourth-quarter net financial loss of $(1.0) million, compared with net financial loss of $(0.8) million for the same period in fiscal 2025. Fiscal 2026 net financial loss totaled $(0.1) million, compared with net financial loss of $(0.4) million in fiscal 2025.

Capital Expenditures and Cash Flows:
•
Fiscal 2026 capital expenditures, including accruals, were $734.3 million compared with $752.5 million in fiscal 2025.
•
NJR expects to deploy between $5.3 billion and $5.7 billion in capital expenditures from fiscal 2027 through fiscal 2031, with utility spending at NJNG representing over 60% of the investment and strategic growth opportunities at CEV and S&T supporting long-term value creation.
•
During fiscal 2026, cash flows from operations increased to $641.2 million, compared to cash flows from operations of $466.3 million in fiscal 2025, due primarily to an increase in financial margin at ES.

About New Jersey Resources

New Jersey Resources (NYSE: NJR) is a diversified energy infrastructure and energy services company headquartered in Wall, New Jersey.

NJR is composed of five primary businesses:

•
New Jersey Natural Gas, NJR’s principal subsidiary, operates and maintains natural gas transportation and distribution infrastructure to serve customers in New Jersey’s Monmouth, Ocean, Morris, Middlesex, Sussex and Burlington counties.

•
Clean Energy Ventures invests in, owns and operates solar projects, providing customers with low-carbon solutions.

•
Energy Services manages a diversified portfolio of natural gas transportation and storage assets and provides physical natural gas services and customized energy solutions to its customers across North America.

•
Storage and Transportation serves customers from local distributors and producers to electric generators and wholesale marketers through its ownership of Leaf River and the Adelphia Gateway pipeline, as well as our 50% equity ownership in the Steckman Ridge natural gas storage facility.

•
Home Services provides service contracts as well as heating, central air conditioning, water heaters, standby generators and other indoor and outdoor comfort products to residential homes throughout New Jersey.

NJR and its almost 1,400 employees are committed to helping customers save energy and money by promoting conservation and encouraging efficiency through Conserve to Preserve® and initiatives such as SAVEGREEN®.

Non-GAAP Financial Information:
This earnings release includes the non-GAAP financial measures NFE/net financial loss, NFE per basic share, financial margin and utility gross margin. A reconciliation of these non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable financial measures calculated and reported in accordance with GAAP can be found below. As an indicator of NJR’s operating performance, these measures should not be considered an alternative to, or more meaningful than, net income or operating revenues as determined in accordance with GAAP. This information has been provided pursuant to the requirements of SEC Regulation G.

NFE and financial margin exclude unrealized gains or losses on derivative instruments related to NJR’s unregulated subsidiaries and certain realized gains and losses on derivative instruments related to natural gas that has been placed into storage at ES, net of applicable tax adjustments as described below. Financial margin also differs from gross margin as defined on a GAAP basis as it excludes certain operations and maintenance expense and depreciation and amortization expenses as well as the effects of derivatives as discussed above. Volatility associated with the change in value of these financial instruments and physical commodity reported on the income statement in the current period. In order to manage its business, NJR views its results without the impacts of the unrealized gains and losses, and certain realized gains and losses, caused by changes in value of these financial instruments and physical commodity contracts prior to the completion of the planned transaction because it shows changes in value currently instead of when the planned transaction ultimately is settled. An annual estimated effective tax rate is calculated for NFE purposes and any necessary quarterly tax adjustment is applied to ES.

NJNG’s utility gross margin is defined as operating revenues less natural gas purchases, sales tax and regulatory rider expenses. This measure differs from gross margin as presented on a GAAP basis as it excludes certain operations and maintenance expense and depreciation and amortization. Utility gross margin may also not be comparable to the definition of gross margin used by others in the natural gas distribution business and other industries. Management believes that utility gross margin provides a meaningful basis for evaluating utility operations since natural gas costs, sales tax and regulatory rider expenses are included in operating revenues and passed through to customers and, therefore, have no effect on utility gross margin.

Management uses these non-GAAP financial measures as supplemental measures to other GAAP results to provide a more complete understanding of NJR’s performance. Management believes these non-GAAP financial measures are more reflective of NJR’s business model, provide transparency to investors and enable period-to-period comparability of financial performance.
