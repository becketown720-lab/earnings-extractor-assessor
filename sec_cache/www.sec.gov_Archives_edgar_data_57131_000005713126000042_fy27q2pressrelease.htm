EXHIBIT 99.1

La-Z-Boy Incorporated Reports Solid Second Quarter Results; Strong Sales Growth In Both Retail And Core North America La-Z-Boy Wholesale Businesses

Fiscal 2027 Second Quarter Highlights:
•Retail segment written sales increased 11%
•Retail delivered sales increased 14%
–Added three company-owned stores including one new and two acquired stores
–Signed agreement to acquire one additional independent La-Z-Boy store
•Wholesale delivered sales decreased 2% versus prior year, which is an increase of 3% excluding the impact of the wholesale casegoods divestiture
•Delivered consolidated sales of $526 million increased 1% versus prior year, which is an increase of 4% excluding the impact of the wholesale casegoods divestiture
•GAAP diluted EPS of $0.45 and adjusted(1) diluted EPS of $0.53
•Returned $32 million to shareholders through share repurchases and dividends, triple the prior year second quarter amount
•Quarterly dividend increased 12% to $0.27, the 6th consecutive year of double-digit increases

MONROE, Mich., November 17, 2026 -- La-Z-Boy Incorporated (NYSE: LZB), a global leader in the retail and manufacture of residential furniture, today reported second quarter results for the period ended October 24, 2026. For the quarter, sales totaled $526 million, growing 1% against the prior year comparable period, or 4% excluding the impact of the wholesale casegoods divestiture completed in May, with strong growth in both Retail and core North America La-Z-Boy wholesale sales. Operating margin was 4.1% for the quarter on a GAAP basis and 5.0% on an adjusted(1) basis. Diluted earnings per share totaled $0.45 on a GAAP basis and $0.53 on an adjusted(1) basis.

Retail segment (company-owned La-Z-Boy stores) delivered sales grew a strong 14%; GAAP and adjusted operating margins were 9.7%. Total written sales for the Retail segment were up 11% versus a year ago driven by growth in acquired stores and new stores. Written same-store sales (which exclude the impact of both newly opened stores and newly acquired stores) declined slightly for the quarter on a year-over-year basis. Positive same-store sales through the first two months of the quarter were offset by significantly softer consumer shopping behavior in October, but have improved in the early weeks of the third quarter.

Melinda D. Whittington, Board Chair, President and Chief Executive Officer of La-Z-Boy Incorporated, said, “We were pleased with the strong sales growth we delivered in the quarter and the continued momentum in our business as we invest for the long-term and execute our Century Vision strategy. Our focus on bringing innovative, high-quality, handcrafted products to market and delivering an inspiring end-to-end shopping experience from online to in-store is strengthening connections with consumers and introducing La-Z-Boy to new audiences. I am confident in our ability to continue to grow our business even in this dynamic environment.”



Whittington added, “As we celebrate La-Z-Boy's 100th anniversary later this year and our legacy of delivering comfortable, customized furniture with quick speed to market, we remain focused on building on our heritage to drive the next century of growth. We are making strategic investments across the enterprise to expand our company-owned Retail footprint through new stores and acquisitions of independent La-Z-Boy stores, enhance our end-to-end consumer experience through digital capabilities and consumer-first innovation, and optimize our manufacturing and distribution operations. We believe the strength of our iconic brand, retail operations, and vertically integrated supply chain position us well to navigate near-term headwinds while investing for long-term sustainable growth.”

Third Quarter Outlook:
Taylor Luebke, SVP and Chief Financial Officer of La-Z-Boy Incorporated, said, “We expect fiscal third quarter sales to be in the range of $505-525 million and adjusted operating margin(2) in the range of 4.0-5.5%, reflecting a measured view of the macroeconomic backdrop, uneven consumer demand, and our continued near-term strategic investments and transitional costs as we optimize our plant footprint and progress our distribution and home delivery transformation.”

Fiscal 2027 Second Quarter Results versus Fiscal 2026 Second Quarter:
•Consolidated sales in the second quarter of Fiscal 2027 increased 1% to $526 million versus last year. Excluding the impact of our wholesale casegoods divestiture, sales increased 4% with growth in our Retail and core North America La-Z-Boy wholesale business
•Consolidated GAAP operating margin was 4.1% versus 6.9% last year
–Consolidated adjusted(1) operating margin was 5.0% versus 7.1% last year, with the change driven by strategic investments in our supply chain including plant consolidations and distribution transformation in progress and a 110 basis point impact from a one-time change in our dealer warranty arrangements which benefitted last year’s second quarter
•GAAP diluted EPS was $0.45 versus $0.70 in the prior year period, and adjusted(1) diluted EPS of $0.53 versus $0.71 in the prior comparable period

Retail Segment:
•Sales:
–Total written sales for the Retail segment (company-owned La-Z-Boy stores) increased 11% compared to the year ago period driven by growth in acquired stores and new stores
▪Written same-store sales (which exclude the impact of new and acquired stores) declined slightly for the quarter versus prior year. Positive comps in the combined first two months of the quarter were more than offset by softening trends in October, but have again strengthened in the early weeks of the third quarter
–Delivered sales increased 14% to $252 million, primarily due to growth from acquired and new stores
•Operating Margin:
–GAAP operating margin was 9.7% versus 10.7%
▪Adjusted(1) operating margin was 9.7% versus 10.7%, driven by a shift in product mix along with targeted promotional activity

Wholesale Segment:
•Sales:
–Sales decreased 2% to $362 million versus last year on a reported basis, which is an increase of 3% excluding the impact of the wholesale casegoods divestiture, driven by growth in our core North America La-Z-Boy wholesale business
•Operating Margin:
–GAAP operating margin was 5.0% versus 7.9%
■Adjusted(1) operating margin was 5.9% versus 8.1%, driven by strategic investments in our supply chain including plant consolidations and distribution transformation in progress and a 150 basis point impact from a one-time change in our dealer warranty arrangements which benefitted last year’s second quarter, partially offset by favorable tariff impact, including refunds and pricing actions net of tariff costs

Corporate & Other:
•Joybird written sales decreased 30% and delivered sales decreased 29% to $25 million on lower delivered volume and reduced marketing investment against a challenged consumer backdrop
•Corporate & Other operating loss and adjusted(1) operating loss increased versus the prior year, primarily due to lower Joybird delivered sales

About La-Z-Boy:
La-Z-Boy Incorporated (NYSE: LZB) is a leading vertically integrated retailer and manufacturer of high-quality, custom furniture that transforms the home. Founded on American heritage, the iconic La-Z-Boy brand has been synonymous with comfort, quality, and craftsmanship for 100 years. As an end-to-end enterprise, the company manages every aspect of its business—from retail, manufacturing, and design to distribution and after-service care.

La-Z-Boy Incorporated brings timeless and modern furniture to life through a retail network of approximately 380 La-Z-Boy stores, including 237 company-owned locations, and its digital platform at La-Z-Boy.com. Within the Wholesale segment, the company manufactures comfortable, high quality, custom furniture, with over 95% of its products produced in North America. Its Joybird® brand is an omnichannel retailer and manufacturer of modern, custom upholstered furniture, operating 19 U.S. stores. With a global team of about 10,000 employees, La-Z-Boy Incorporated was named to TIME’s 2026 list of America’s Most Iconic Companies and Newsweek’s 2025 list of America’s Best Retailers, ranking No. 1 in the furniture category. The company continues to shape the way people live by delivering the transformational power of comfort.

Adjusted Financial Measures:
In addition to the financial measures prepared in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (“GAAP”), this press release also includes adjusted financial measures. Management uses these adjusted financial measures when assessing our ongoing performance. This press release contains references to adjusted operating income (loss) (on a consolidated basis and by segment), adjusted operating margin (on a consolidated basis and by segment), and adjusted net income attributable to La-Z-Boy Incorporated per diluted share, adjusted diluted earnings per share (and components thereof, including adjusted income before income taxes and adjusted net income attributable to La-Z-Boy Incorporated), each of which may exclude, as applicable, supply chain optimization charges, business realignment charges, purchase accounting charges, distribution and home delivery transformation charges, and pension termination charges. The supply chain optimization charges in Fiscal 2027 include severance costs, accelerated depreciation, accelerated lease expense, and the impairment of long-lived assets, all of which relate to closure of manufacturing facilities in Mexico. The business realignment charges in Fiscal 2027 include a loss on the divestiture of our Casegoods wholesale business along with severance expense, accelerated lease expense and lease impairment charges associated with the former Casegoods wholesale business. The purchase accounting charges include the amortization of intangible assets and incremental expense upon the sale of inventory acquired at fair value. The distribution and home delivery transformation charges include accelerated lease expense, severance costs, other costs, and the impairment of long-lived assets, all associated with exiting former distribution centers. The pension termination charges include one-time charges related to the termination and settlement of our non-qualified legacy acquired retirement plan. The press release also includes sales figures excluding the wholesale casegoods divestiture. These adjusted financial measures are not meant to be considered superior to or a substitute for La-Z-Boy Incorporated’s results of operations prepared in accordance with GAAP and may not be comparable to similarly titled measures reported by other companies. Reconciliations of such adjusted financial measures to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures are set forth in the accompanying tables.

Management believes that presenting certain adjusted financial measures will help investors understand the long-term profitability trends of our business and compare our profitability to prior and future periods and to our peers. Management excludes purchase accounting charges because the amount and timing of such charges are significantly impacted by the timing, size, number and nature of the acquisitions consummated. While the company has a history of acquisition activity, it does not acquire businesses on a predictable cycle, and the impact of purchase accounting charges are unique to each acquisition and can vary significantly from acquisition to acquisition. Similarly, distribution and home delivery transformation charges, business realignment charges, and supply chain optimization charges are dependent on the timing, size, number and nature of the operations being opened or closed, consolidated or centralized, and the charges may not be incurred on a predictable cycle. Management also excludes pension termination charges due to the infrequent nature of such transactions. Management believes that exclusion of these items facilitates more consistent comparisons of the company’s operating results over time. Where applicable, the accompanying “Reconciliation of GAAP to Adjusted Financial Measures” tables present the excluded items net of tax calculated using the effective tax rate from operations for the period in which the adjustment is presented.
