Bath & Body Works Reports Third Quarter Results Exceeding Guidance and Raises Full-Year Sales Outlook

•Delivers Q3 net sales and adjusted earnings per share above guidance
•Third quarter net sales of $1.6 billion, down 1.0%. Earnings per diluted share of $0.19; adjusted earnings per diluted share of $0.26
•Executing Consumer First Formula with continued progress in digital, customer engagement and expanded distribution
•Announces new $500 million share repurchase program
•Raises full-year 2026 net sales guidance to a decline of 3.4% to 2.1%, updating reported earnings per diluted share guidance to $3.09 to $3.29 and maintaining adjusted earnings per diluted share guidance of $2.60 to $2.80
COLUMBUS, Ohio - November 19, 2026 - Bath & Body Works, Inc. (NYSE: BBWI) today reported third quarter 2026 results.
Daniel Heaf, chief executive officer of Bath & Body Works, commented, “Our third quarter results exceeded our sales and adjusted earnings per share guidance, and marks encouraging progress in our Consumer First Formula. One year after introducing this strategy, we are seeing our actions translate into results across product, brand, and marketplace.”
“Our product innovation is resonating with customers and digital delivered its second consecutive quarter of growth. Our wholesale business with Ulta and Amazon continues to gain momentum and we are telling bigger and bolder brand stories through a more coordinated approach to marketing. While these are encouraging early proof points, we have more work to do to drive consistent growth across our channels.”
“We are one year into a multi-year transformation, and progress will not always be linear. As we approach the important holiday season, we are taking a prudent view of the consumer environment while remaining focused on execution. Our priority is to scale what is working, address the areas that need improvement and build a foundation for sustainable growth in 2027.”
Third Quarter 2026 Results
The company reported net sales of $1,577 million for the quarter ended October 31, 2026, a decrease of 1.0% compared to net sales of $1,594 million for the quarter ended November 1, 2025.
Earnings per diluted share were $0.19 for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $0.37 last year. Third quarter operating income was $108 million compared to $161 million last year, and net income was $38 million compared to $77 million last year.
Reported third quarter 2026 results included aggregate pre-tax costs of $13 million ($10 million after tax) associated with business transformation activities and a $5 million pre-tax loss ($4 million after tax) related to the extinguishment of outstanding debt. Excluding these items, adjusted earnings per diluted share for the third quarter of 2026 was $0.26, adjusted operating income was $121 million and adjusted net income was $52 million.
Reported third quarter 2025 results included an $8 million pre-tax gain ($6 million after tax) associated with the sale of a non-core asset. Excluding this item, adjusted earnings per diluted share for the third quarter of 2025 was $0.35 and adjusted net income was $71 million.
At the conclusion of this press release is a reconciliation of reported-to-adjusted results, including a description of the adjusted items.
2026 Guidance
Based on third quarter performance and the company’s current outlook for the fourth quarter, the company is increasing its full-year 2026 net sales guidance to a decline between 3.4% and 2.1% compared to $7,291 million in fiscal 2025. The company is also updating full-year reported earnings per diluted share guidance to between $3.09 and $3.29, compared to $3.11 in fiscal 2025, and maintaining adjusted earnings per diluted share guidance of between $2.60 and $2.80, compared to $3.21 in fiscal 2025.

                    
For the fourth quarter of 2026, the company is forecasting net sales to decline between 5.5% and 2.0% compared to $2,724 million in the fourth quarter of 2025. Fourth quarter 2026 earnings per diluted share is expected to be between $1.41 and $1.61, compared to earnings per diluted share of $1.99 and adjusted earnings per diluted share of $2.05 in the fourth quarter of 2025.
At the conclusion of this press release is a reconciliation of guidance-to-adjusted guidance, including a description of the adjusted items.
For a reconciliation of our reported GAAP to adjusted non-GAAP earnings per diluted share for the full-year and fourth quarter of 2025, refer to our Current Report on Form 8-K, filed with the SEC on March 4, 2026.
Stock Repurchase Authorization
Bath & Body Works, Inc. also announced today that the Board of Directors approved a new share repurchase program authorizing the company to repurchase up to $500 million of the company’s outstanding shares of common stock, which may commence upon completion of the company’s existing January 2025 program.

ABOUT BATH & BODY WORKS
Bath & Body Works is a global leader in personal care and home fragrance, driven by the belief that everybody deserves to feel good.
The brand’s beloved and iconic scents are expertly crafted for exceptional performance and a luxury fragrance experience. Formulated with thoughtfully chosen ingredients, Bath & Body Works’ body care products are available in multiple forms including fine fragrance mist, body cream, lotion, eau de parfum, body wash, hand soap, sanitizer and more. The brand’s famous 3-wick candles are made with rich, high-quality fragrance oils layered throughout a premium soy wax base, for up to 45 hours of room-filling fragrance.
Consumers can shop Bath & Body Works anytime and anywhere they choose, from welcoming, in-store experiences at more than 1,900 stores in the U.S. and Canada, 550-plus international locations and select Ulta Beauty stores. Online, consumers can visit bathandbodyworks.com, Amazon and Ulta.com.
