Third Quarter 2026 Highlights and Key Developments

•
Total loans grew 2.2% from the end of the linked quarter and 5.8% from September 30, 2025, to reach $4.86 billion at September 30, 2026, driven by robust commercial lending.
•
Total deposits increased 3.3% quarter-over-quarter and 2.2% year-over-year to $5.47 billion at September 30, 2026. Growth was broad-based across all deposit categories, led by seasonal support in public deposits.
•
Net interest income reached a new quarterly high of $54.3 million and net interest margin of 3.70% was stable with the linked quarter and up 5 basis points from the year-ago quarter.
•
Investment advisory income, the Company's leading contributor of noninterest income, increased 1.7% and 10.6% from the linked and year-ago quarters, respectively.
•
The efficiency ratio remained below 57% for the quarter, reflecting the Company's focus on sustained profitability.
•
The Company's strong capital position enabled the repurchase of 185,833 shares of common stock at an average price of $40.92 per share during the quarter.

"Our third quarter results underscore the strength of our relationship-driven business model and our unwavering commitment to disciplined execution," said President and Chief Executive Officer Martin K. Birmingham. "Our commercial franchise continues to be the primary driver of loan growth and was supported by increases in consumer indirect and residential mortgage lending during the third quarter. While our quarterly net charge-off ratio was up from lower-than-typical levels in the linked and year-ago quarters, it remains within our full-year guidance. At the same time, the ratio of non-performing loans to total loans decreased by 3 basis points. Overall, we remain focused on credit-disciplined loan production in our core Upstate New York markets."

Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer W. Jack Plants II added, "Third quarter performance benefited from continued growth in average earning assets and effective balance sheet management, supporting a 3.70% net interest margin and a nearly 5% increase in net interest income from the prior year period. Our balance sheet remains a source of strength, reflected in a tangible common equity ratio(1) of 8.40%, a common equity Tier 1 ratio of 11.36%, and a return on average tangible common equity(1) of 13.75%. These strong capital levels provide us flexibility to support loan growth and thoughtful investments in our business, while continuing to return capital to shareholders through share buybacks, as we did during the third quarter with the repurchase of 185,833 shares."
