EXHIBIT 99.1

HORIZON KINETICS HOLDING CORPORATION REPORTS THIRD QUARTER RESULTS

 

Highlights for the quarter ended September 30, 2026:

 

•
Management and advisory fees were $17.8 million for each of the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025. The quarter included a 26% increase in revenues from the Company's exchange-traded funds (ETFs). Management and advisory fees from separately managed accounts (SMAs) grew 2.0% for the quarter and 4.3% year-to-date.
•
Operating income was $1.8 million (GAAP presentation) and $4.3 million advisor only presentation (Non-GAAP) for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 reflecting the Company's core asset management operations.
•
Net income (loss) attributable to Horizon Kinetics Holding Corporation was a net loss of $18.4 million, or $0.98 per common share, and net income of $35.8 million, or $1.92 per share, for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, respectively. The net loss for the quarter was the result of unrealized declines in the fair value of certain key investment holdings, including those at consolidated investment products, while the year-to-date period continued to reflect unrealized gains at those same investment holdings.
•
Assets under management (AUM) was $10.0 billion as of September 30, 2026, a decrease of 7.3% from June 30, 2026. AUM remains higher than the beginning of the year value of $9.6 billion, reflecting an increase of 3.9% from December 31, 2025.
•
Board of Directors declared a $0.13 per share dividend.
•
The Company’s Board of Directors authorized a $25.0 million share repurchase program.

 

 

New York, NY – November 12, 2026

 

Horizon Kinetics Holding Corporation (the “Company” or “HKHC”) (OTCQX: HKHC) reported financial results for its third quarter of 2026.

 

The Company's management and advisory fees increased by 0.3% for the third quarter of 2026 and were down 1.2% for the year-to-date period. The Company continued to experience growth from our Inflation Beneficiaries ETF, whose revenues increased 26% for the quarter and 30% for the year-to-date period. However, these increases were more than offset by decreased revenues from our mutual funds. The Company also reported revenue increases of 2.0% for the quarter and 4.3% for the year-to-date period from separately managed accounts.

 

The Company's AUM was $10.0 billion, an increase of 3.9% for the year-to-date period primarily due to the increase in market value of Texas Pacific Land Corporation ("TPL"), which increased 13.4% year-to-date. The impact of the TPL increase was partially offset by decreases in bitcoin-related holdings. The Company has also experienced net outflows for the year-to-date period primarily from redemptions from private funds holding shares of Miami International Holdings, Inc., which were generally expected due to the expiration of restrictions following its IPO during 2025.

 

The Company’s total operating expenses increased $1.3 million, or 8.9%, during the third quarter as a result of higher general and administrative expenses associated with the commencement of new replacement office leases, which includes an impairment of the right-of-use asset and a period of overlapping lease terms associated with our New York location. These increased costs were partially offset by lower variable compensation.

 

As described in the advisor only presentation (Non-GAAP), HKHC shareholders experienced declines from a $19.8 million net loss from equity in earnings of private funds, $19.5 million of unrealized losses, net, from investments, offset with $3.8 million of unrealized gains from digital asset holdings for the three months ended September 30, 2026. These results partially offset significant unrealized gains from the first and second quarters and have resulted in total other income of $20.5 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

On November 11, 2026, the Company's Board of Directors declared a cash dividend of $0.13 per share, payable on December 10, 2026, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on November 23, 2026. Also, the Company’s Board of Directors announced today that the Board of Directors have unanimously approved a 3-year stock repurchase program ("Program") under which the Company may repurchase up to $25 million of the Company's outstanding common stock. Purchases under the Program may be made through a combination of open market purchases in compliance with Rule 10b-18 under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, block trades, privately negotiated transactions or other transactions in the discretion of the Company. The Company may also, from time to time, enter into Rule 10b5-1 plans to facilitate repurchases under this authorization. The volume, price, timing and manner of any repurchases will be determined at the Company's discretion, subject to general market conditions and regulatory factors. The Program does not obligate the Company to repurchase any specific amount of common stock and may be modified, suspended or discontinued at any time without notice at the discretion of the Company. The Company expects to fund the Program from existing cash and cash equivalents.

Additional Information about our performance

 

The Company consolidates certain private funds in order for the condensed consolidated financial statements to conform with generally accepted accounting principles. As a result, the assets and liabilities of the applicable consolidated funds are presented on the Company’s consolidated statements of financial condition. Additionally, an amount that represents the interests of the Company’s clients’ in these consolidated private funds will be presented as redeemable noncontrolling interests on the Company’s consolidated statements of financial condition. The investment income (losses), other income (losses) and the expenses of the consolidated investment products will be presented within the Company’s consolidated statements of operations. Additionally, an amount that represents the net income attributable to redeemable noncontrolling interests as well as the net income (loss) attributable to Horizon Kinetics Holding Corporation will be presented on the Company’s condensed consolidated statement of operations.

 

Consolidated Investment Products (“CIPs”) consist of certain private investment funds which are sponsored by the Company. The Company has no right to the CIPs’ assets, other than its direct equity investments in them and investment management and other fees earned from them. The liabilities of the CIPs have no recourse to the Company’s assets beyond the level of its direct investment, therefore the Company bears no other risks associated with the CIPs’ liabilities.

 

As indicated in the additional information presented in the tables below there are several notable presentational differences as a result of the consolidation of the CIPs:

 

•
Management and advisory fees, including incentive fees, from CIPs are eliminated from consolidated revenues. Accordingly, our presentation without the CIPs reflects higher revenues for the three- and nine-month periods than the GAAP presentation. There was management and advisory fee growth of $16.7 million, a 27% increase, for the nine months ended September 30, 2026. This increase is primarily the result of the $18.1 million of incentive fees recorded in the first quarter of 2026 related to certain private funds that held Miami International Holdings, Inc. and the expiration of certain trading restrictions associated with that position.

 

•
The equity in earnings of private funds which results primarily from CIPs that are eliminated from the consolidated presentation is included within the investment results of the CIPs. Accordingly, our presentation without the CIPs reflects an increased level of equity earnings that presents an increase in the value of our holdings within the CIPs.

 

•
Shareholders’ equity and net income attributable to Horizon Kinetics Holding Corporation are not impacted by the consolidation process.

 

•
The Statement of Financial Condition without the consolidation of private funds presents lower total assets as a result of excluding the assets held by the CIPs as well as the associated redeemable noncontrolling interests, which represents our clients’ interests in these funds. As of September 30, 2026, the Company holds economic interests of $219.5 million in the CIPs, which is reflected in Other Investments in the advisor only (Non-GAAP) condensed consolidated statements of financial condition.
