A. O. Smith Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
(Comparisons are year-over-year (“YoY”), unless otherwise noted)
•Sales of $941 million; net earnings of $126 million and diluted earnings per share (EPS) of $0.93
•North America segment sales of $761.6 million increased 3% driven by Leonard Valve acquisition sales, 13% boiler sales growth and the benefits of pricing actions, partially offset by lower residential water heater volumes
•Rest of World segment sales of $192.8 million decreased 7%, reflecting continued weakness in China's consumer appliance market
•Year-to-date operating cash flow was $429 million and free cash flow increased 4% to $396 million
•Concluded China strategic assessment with definitive agreements, announced October 27, under which CPE will acquire an 81% interest in the Company's China business; with A. O. Smith retaining a 19% stake and long-term brand license
•2026 full year guidance has been updated to exclude the China business for all of 2026:
◦Net sales growth, excluding China, of approximately 4%
◦Adjusted EPS1, excluding China, of between $3.42 and $3.52

Milwaukee, Wis.— Global water technology company A. O. Smith Corporation ("A. O. Smith" or “the Company”) (NYSE: AOS) today announced its third quarter 2026 results.

“Disciplined execution across our businesses remained a priority in the third quarter as we navigated persistent inflationary pressures, softer residential water heater demand in North America, and ongoing market challenges in China,” said Steve Shafer, chairman and chief executive officer. “Our teams adapted to changing market conditions, managed costs and stayed focused on meeting our customers’ needs. Supported by our leading market positions and financial strength, we believe we remain well positioned to address near-term headwinds and deliver long-term, profitable growth.”
"Separately, earlier this week we announced definitive agreements with CPE to acquire an 81% interest in our China business," Shafer continued. "Our team in China has built a leading premium brand over more than three decades, and CPE is well positioned to lead that business forward. For A. O. Smith, this transaction completes our strategic assessment and enables us to focus our capital and resources on our highest-return opportunities and sustainable value creation. At the same time, our retained 19% stake and long-term brand license give our shareholders the opportunity to benefit from future growth in China under CPE's leadership."
Segment-level Performance
North America
Third quarter sales increased 3% to $761.6 million, driven by higher boiler volumes, pricing benefits and a $15 million sales contribution from Leonard Valve, acquired in January 2026, partially offset by lower residential water heater volumes. Excluding Leonard Valve, organic sales increased 1%.
Segment earnings were $179.9 million, and segment margin was 23.6% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to segment earnings of $179.7 million and segment margin of 24.2% in the same period of 2025. The 60-basis-point decline in segment margin was primarily due to lower residential water heater volumes and higher input costs, including steel and freight, more than offsetting the benefits of higher boiler volumes, International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA) tariff refunds, the benefits of pricing actions and the earnings contribution from Leonard Valve.
Rest of World
Rest of World sales of $192.8 million decreased 7% compared to the prior year period and included a favorable currency translation impact of $5 million primarily related to sales in China. China sales decreased 17% in local currency, reflecting continued weak consumer demand and disruption related to the recently concluded strategic assessment process.
Segment earnings were $10.5 million, and segment margin was 5.4% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to segment earnings of $15.4 million and segment margin of 7.4% in the same period of 2025. The lower segment earnings and segment margin compared to the prior year were primarily due to lower China sales volumes, partially offset by continued cost management.



Announced Sale of 81% Interest in China Business
On October 27, 2026, the Company announced definitive agreements with CPE, a leading alternative asset manager, to acquire an 81% interest in A. O. Smith’s China business. A. O. Smith will retain a 19% stake and enter into a long-term brand license agreement, both of which allow it to participate in the business's future growth. The transaction values the China business at approximately $400 million on a cash-free, debt-free basis, before taking into account the long-term brand licensing royalty stream. The total value, including the net present value of the ongoing licensing economics payable to A. O. Smith over the multi-decade period, is expected to exceed $500 million. In addition, the transaction structure will enable A. O. Smith to repatriate approximately $80 million in cash that is currently being held in China. Consistent with its commitment to returning capital to shareholders, the Company intends to prioritize share repurchases as a use of the after-tax proceeds and repatriated cash following the closing of the transaction.
The agreements follow a comprehensive review of strategic alternatives for the China business, announced in July 2025. After evaluating a broad range of options, A. O. Smith’s Board of Directors concluded that a strategic transaction with CPE is the best path forward for the China business, the Company and its shareholders. The transaction is expected to close in the first half of 2027, subject to regulatory approvals and other customary closing conditions.
Balance Sheet, Liquidity and Capital Allocation
As of September 30, 2026, cash balances totaled $234.5 million and debt totaled $673.1 million, resulting in a leverage ratio of 27.0% as measured by total debt-to-total capitalization. The increased leverage ratio compared to 2025 was due to cash borrowed under a new term loan used to acquire Leonard Valve in January 2026.
Cash provided by operations was $429.4 million in the first nine months of 2026, slightly lower than the same period in 2025. Free cash flow in the first nine months of 2026 increased 4% to $396.2 million, supported by lower capital expenditures of $33.2 million compared with $53.2 million in the prior year period. Higher non-cash expense adjustments and improved working capital offset lower earnings.
As part of its commitment to return capital to shareholders, the Company deployed $260.6 million to repurchase 4.2 million shares in the first nine months of 2026. As of September 30, 2026, authority remained to repurchase approximately 1.6 million additional shares. The Company projects its full-year 2026 share repurchases to total approximately $300 million.
On October 12, 2026, the Company’s board of directors approved a 6% increase in the dividend rate, resulting in a five-year compound annual dividend growth rate of approximately 7%. The Company has increased its dividend for more than 30 consecutive years. For the full release, click here.



2026 Outlook
As a result of the announced sale of a majority interest in the China business, which represents a strategic shift in the Company’s portfolio, the Company expects to classify the China business as held for sale and report it as discontinued operations beginning in the fourth quarter of 2026, with prior periods recast accordingly. Third quarter 2026 results presented in this release include the China business. Upon closing of the transaction, the Company expects to deconsolidate the China business and account for its retained 19% interest under the equity method. The Company will also review its segment reporting structure during the fourth quarter of 2026.
Accordingly, the Company has updated its full-year 2026 guidance to exclude the China business for all of 2026.

About A. O. Smith
A. O. Smith Corporation, with headquarters in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, is a global leader applying innovative technology and energy-efficient solutions to products manufactured and marketed worldwide. Listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE: AOS), the Company is one of the world’s leading manufacturers of residential and commercial water heating equipment and boilers, as well as a manufacturer of water treatment and water management products.
