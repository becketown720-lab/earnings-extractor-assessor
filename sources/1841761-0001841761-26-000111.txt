Grove Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
SAN FRANCISCO, CA — November 10, 2026 — Grove Collaborative Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: GROV) (“Grove” or the “Company”), the world’s first plastic neutral retailer and a leading sustainable consumer products company, certified B Corporation, and Public Benefit Corporation, today reported financial results for its fiscal third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Key Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights:
●Total Net Revenue was $36.8 million, down 15.8% year-over-year, but up 0.7% sequentially — the second consecutive quarter of sequential Net Revenue growth.
●Adjusted EBITDA was positive $1.4 million, compared to negative $1.2 million in the same period last year, the fourth consecutive quarter of positive Adjusted EBITDA.*
●Net Income was $0.1 million, compared to a Net Loss of $3.0 million in the same period last year.*
●Operating Cash Flow was positive $0.2 million, compared to negative $1.0 million in the same period last year.*
●Narrowing full-year guidance to Net Revenue of approximately $144 million to $146 million (previously $142.5 million to $152.5 million) and Adjusted EBITDA of approximately $1 million to $2 million* (previously breakeven to positive low single digit millions).
*Adjusted EBITDA, Net Income and Operating Cash Flow for the third quarter of 2026, includes the benefit of a $1.2 million refund of previously paid tariffs received in the third quarter, which reduced cost of goods sold. Tariff expense was included in Adjusted EBITDA when it was incurred, and the Company therefore has not adjusted the refund out of Adjusted EBITDA.
“Third quarter results reflect the operating discipline we have built over the past several quarters. Net Revenue grew sequentially for the second consecutive quarter to $36.8 million. We also delivered positive Adjusted EBITDA for the fourth consecutive quarter, and delivered positive Adjusted EBITDA and net income in the same quarter for the first time since becoming a publicly-traded company,” said Jeff Yurcisin, Chief Executive Officer of Grove Collaborative. “Our focus remains on returning Grove to revenue growth while building a durable, profitable business grounded in customer trust that rests on curation, ingredient standards, and product vetting that no ecommerce player rivals. We have delivered a
meaningfully improved customer experience, a new subscription experience, and a larger curated offering of healthier-home categories through our dropship capabilities. We are doing this with human health at the center, building the trusted destination for families who care about what comes into their home while maintaining the expense discipline we have demonstrated in prior quarters.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
(All comparisons are versus the quarter ended September 30, 2025 except where otherwise noted)
Net Revenue was $36.8 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, a decline of 15.8% year-over-year, but an increase of 0.7% compared to the second quarter of 2026. The year-over-year decline was primarily driven by a smaller active customer base than a year ago, reflecting lower advertising investment over the past year, and impact from the 2025 disruptions on its ecommerce platform. The effects of the 2025 platform disruptions and lower advertising investment in prior periods are expected to have a diminishing impact on year-over-year comparisons in 2027. The sequential increase was driven by QVC revenue, including $1.2 million from a Today’s Special Value program, partially offset by a slight decline in DTC net revenue.
Gross Margin was 55.8%, an increase of 250 basis points compared to 53.3% in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was driven primarily by the tariff refund and more targeted promotional spend, partially offset by a larger mix of QVC revenue during the quarter, which contributed a lower margin compared to the Company’s DTC business.
Operating Expenses were $20.3 million, a decrease of 22.1% compared to $26.1 million in the prior-year period. The decline reflects lower fulfillment costs driven by lower order volume and lower outbound shipping rates, lower personnel-related expenses from reduced headcount, and reduced advertising spend.
Net Income was $0.1 million, or 0.3% Net Income margin, compared to a net loss of $3.0 million, or (6.8%) Net Loss margin, in the prior-year period. The year-over-year improvement reflects lower operating expenses and the tariff refund, offset by the decline in revenue.
Adjusted EBITDA was positive $1.4 million, or 3.7% margin, compared to negative $1.2 million, or (2.7%) margin, in the prior-year period. Adjusted EBITDA includes the $1.2 million tariff refund. This
marks the fourth consecutive quarter of positive Adjusted EBITDA and reflects continued operating discipline as the Company invests in the customer experience.
Operating Cash Flow was positive $0.2 million for the quarter, reflecting positive net income net of non-cash expenses, inclusive of the tariff refund, partially offset by a net use of working capital, primarily from the timing of inventory purchases, as an increase in inventory was largely offset by an increase in accounts payable. This compares to negative $1.0 million in the prior-year period.
Cash, Cash Equivalents, and Restricted Cash totaled $11.1 million as of September 30, 2026, down from $11.4 million as of June 30, 2026, primarily reflecting capitalized expenditures as a result of continued investment in the Company’s ecommerce platform enhancements and payments on financing agreements, partially offset by positive operating cash flow.

Direct to Consumer (DTC) Total Orders were 473,000, a decline of 23.6% year-over-year. The decrease was primarily driven by a smaller active customer base than a year ago. The lower active customer base was partially offset by an increase in the frequency of orders per active customer in the quarter.
DTC Active Customers – defined as the number of customers that have placed an order in the trailing twelve months – totaled 458,000 as of September 30, 2026, a decrease of 30.6% year-over-year. The decline reflects lower advertising investment and customer attrition associated with the 2025 ecommerce platform disruptions.
DTC Net Revenue Per Order was $70.85, an increase of 6.1% year-over-year. The improvement was driven primarily by continued efficiency in promotional spend following the launch of the Company’s new loyalty program in the fourth quarter of 2025 and larger mix of higher-priced items in customer
orders, reflecting the Company’s continued category expansion into higher priced assortment, in addition to an increase in shipping revenue per order.
Plastic Intensity1 – measured as pounds of plastic per $100 in net revenue across all online and retail sales — was 0.86 pounds in the third quarter of 2026, improving from 0.94 pounds in the third quarter of 2025.
2026 Financial Outlook:
For the twelve-month period ending December 31, 2026, Grove has narrowed its full-year guidance.
●The Company now expects full-year net revenue of approximately $144 million to $146 million, within our prior guidance of $142.5 million to $152.5 million, and Adjusted EBITDA of approximately $1 million to $2 million, within prior guidance of breakeven to positive low single digit millions.
●The Company previously expected sequential net revenue improvement in each of the remaining quarters of 2026. The Company now expects fourth quarter net revenue to be modestly below the third quarter. Third quarter net revenue included $1.2 million from a Today’s Special Value program on QVC, and the Company does not expect a comparable program in the fourth quarter. The Company expects its DTC channel to be approximately flat in the fourth quarter compared to the third quarter reflecting continued stabilization of its DTC business.
